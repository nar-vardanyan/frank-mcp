# AI Customer Interview Software: 12 Tools Compared (2026)

**Last updated:** Nov 27, 2026. Competitor facts accessed Nov 27, 2026.

**Short answer:** AI customer interview software runs one-to-one interviews with an AI moderator that asks your questions, follows up, and summarizes the answers. The right pick depends on who you interview (your own customers or a recruited panel), the format (voice, video or text), and whether prices are published. Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers, has a Free plan, and is [available as an app in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168); Listen Labs, Outset and GetWhy are a better fit when you need a recruited panel or video.

## How to choose

1. **Who do you need to hear from?** If you already have customers to invite, a tool that interviews your own list is enough. If you need people you cannot reach, pick a tool with a panel and check how recruiting is billed.
2. **Which format fits the question?** Voice suits open conversations about decisions and motivations. Video and screen tasks suit usability and ad testing.
3. **Can you see the price?** Some tools publish plans; others quote after a demo.
4. **Where will you read the results?** If your team works in ChatGPT or Claude, check whether the tool connects there.
5. **Run a small pilot.** Take the interview yourself, then invite five to ten real customers before you scale up.

## FAQ

### What is AI customer interview software?

AI customer interview software runs one-to-one interviews with an AI moderator. It asks your questions, follows up on vague answers, and turns many conversations into transcripts, themes and quotes. Tools differ on who you interview (your own customers or a recruited panel), the format (voice, video or text) and whether prices are published.

### Which AI customer interview tools publish prices?

As of Nov 27, 2026: Frank AI Researcher (Starter $49/mo billed annually or $59 monthly), Usercall (Lite $89/mo or $899 yearly), User Intuition ($30 per quality voice interview with your own sample, $60 with its panel), Voicepanel (projects from $5,000) and Dovetail (Bronze, listed as coming soon, $4,800 a year plus credits). Maze publishes a Free plan and custom Enterprise pricing. Listen Labs, Outset, Strella, Conveo, GetWhy and Userology do not list prices on the pages we opened.

### Which tools have a free plan or a free way to start?

Frank's Free plan includes 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month, with no credit card. Maze has a Free plan (1 study a month), but AI-moderated interviews are an Enterprise add-on. Usercall's first interview is free, User Intuition offers 3 free voice interviews with your own participants, Conveo offers a free sign-up, and Dovetail offers a 30-day trial of its Gold plan.

### Which AI interview tools work inside ChatGPT?

Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. Listen Labs' docs say it is available in the OpenAI App Store, and Strella says it is a connector in ChatGPT. Outset, Usercall, User Intuition and Dovetail say their MCP servers connect to ChatGPT. Frank's remote MCP server is on every plan. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse.

### Can I interview my own customers instead of a panel?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher interviews only your own customers, from a link or website widget. Usercall, Listen Labs, Strella, User Intuition and Maze also say you can bring your own participants, and they offer recruiting as well.

### Does Frank AI Researcher recruit participants?

No. Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews with customers you invite. If you need a recruited panel, Listen Labs, Outset, Strella, User Intuition, GetWhy, Userology, Voicepanel and Maze describe recruiting on their own sites.
