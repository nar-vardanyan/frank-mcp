## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand how a landlord, a property manager, or a tenant uses the product, where the workflow gets stuck, or why they would change tools. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite landlords, property managers, or tenants you already have. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [product] for a lease, a work order, a showing, or a rent payment. What were you trying to get done?
2. Are you a landlord, a property manager, or a tenant, and who else has to be involved for this to work?
3. Where did the workflow get stuck, and what did you do instead?
4. What do you check before you trust a property tool with tenant data, payments, or access?
5. Tell me about a moment when a delay, an error, or a missed message caused a problem for you or a tenant.
6. What would make you move this workflow to a different product?
7. After you started using it, what took longer than you expected before it became part of the daily routine?
8. If you could change one thing about how this product fits a property, a manager, or a tenant, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a proptech customer interview?

Ask about a recent lease, work order, or rent payment: who was involved (a landlord, a property manager, or a tenant), where the workflow got stuck, what they check before they trust a tool with tenant data or payments, and what would make them change products. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit proptech customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite landlords, property managers, or tenants you already have.

### Who should I invite: landlords, property managers, or tenants?

Invite people you already have who use the product in that role, not only the contract signer. A landlord, a property manager, and a tenant often see different parts of the same workflow. Frank does not recruit a panel of new participants.
