## How it works

1. Choose deals that just closed, including both wins and losses, from your own CRM or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.
2. Write a short guide: what prompted the search, what options they compared, what mattered in the decision, what almost changed the outcome, and what they use now.
3. Share an interviewer link, or embed a website widget, so buyers can talk when they have time, without booking a call.
4. Read the transcripts and themes. Check any theme you will act on against exact quotes, and look at wins and losses together.

A CRM close reason is useful for counting outcomes. Interviews are for the story behind a short reason code, such as price or missing feature.

### How can I run win/loss interviews on deals we just won and lost?

Invite buyers from deals you just closed, covering both wins and losses, then share an AI-moderated voice interview link so they can talk when they have time. Ask what mattered in the decision, what almost changed the outcome, and what they use now, then read the transcripts and themes.

### Does Frank recruit buyers for win/loss interviews?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite buyers from your own closed deals, for example from your CRM or email list.

### Who should I invite?
