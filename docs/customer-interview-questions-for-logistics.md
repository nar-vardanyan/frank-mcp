## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand how a shipper, a carrier, or an operations manager ran a recent shipment, how an exception was handled, or why they would change providers. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite shippers, carriers, or operations managers you already have. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last shipment or load you ran with [product]. Were you the shipper, the carrier, or an operations manager, and what were you trying to get done?
2. Where did the process break or slow down, and how did you find out?
3. Walk me through a recent exception, delay, or failed handoff. What did you do next?
4. Who else has to be in the loop, and what does each person need to see?
5. What do you check before you trust a logistics tool with a live shipment or a customer commitment?
6. What would make you move this workflow to a different provider?
7. After you started using it, what took longer than you expected before operations relied on it day to day?
8. If you could change one thing about how this product handles a shipment, an exception, or a handoff, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a logistics customer interview?

Ask about a recent shipment or exception: whether the person is a shipper, a carrier, or an operations manager, where the process broke, who else had to be in the loop, and what would make them change providers. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit logistics customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite shippers, carriers, or operations managers you already have.

### Who should I invite: shippers, carriers, or operations managers?

Invite people you already have who ran a recent shipment or exception in that role. A shipper, a carrier, and an operations manager often see different parts of the same handoff. Frank does not recruit a panel of new participants.
