## Why an interview link works for win-loss

- **No sales rep in the conversation.** Buyers are often more candid with someone who is not their rep.
- **No extra meeting.** Buyers talk when they have time, without booking another call.
- **The same guide for every deal,** so wins, losses, and no-decisions can be compared.

## Set up win-loss from ChatGPT

1. **Pick deals that just closed** from your own CRM, including wins, losses, and no-decisions.
2. **Draft the guide in chat:** what prompted the search, which options they compared (including doing nothing), what mattered, what almost changed the outcome, and what they use now. Use a separate version for no-decision deals.
3. **Review and approve.** The study stays a draft until you approve publishing.
4. **Invite buyers,** ideally from someone other than their sales rep, and say plainly that the interview will not reopen the deal.
5. **Read wins and losses together** and check each theme against exact quotes.

## CRM close reason vs the buyer's story

A CRM close reason is useful for counting outcomes. Interviews are for the story behind a short reason code such as price or a missing feature.

```text
From Frank, read the transcripts in my win-loss study. For each deal, compare the buyer's deciding factor, in their words, with this CRM close reason: [paste deal and reason list]. List where they match, where they differ, and two exact quotes for each difference.
```

## Draft prompt

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to interview buyers from deals we lost in the last 60 days. Write 6 to 8 open, vendor-neutral questions: what started the search, which options they considered, who was involved, when their confidence changed, where price came up, and the deciding factor. Wait for me to review the guide. I will send the interviewer link myself.
```

## What it does not do

- **No contacting buyers.** Frank does not recruit participants or reach out to buyers. You send the link.
- **No third-party panel of buyers.** You interview people from your own deals.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **No automatic publishing.** A study drafted in ChatGPT stays a draft until you approve it.

Yes. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. You can draft a win-loss study in chat, send the voice interview link to buyers from recent deals, and read the transcripts and themes in the same chat.

### Who should send the win-loss invite?

Someone other than the buyer's sales rep, such as a product lead or founder. Say plainly that the goal is to learn and that the interview will not reopen the deal.

Yes. Use a separate version of the guide that asks what made proceeding difficult and what the team is doing instead, without assuming another vendor won.
