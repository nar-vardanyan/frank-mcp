## Questions: the last session and the uninstall

Use this group when people deleted the app, or stopped opening it, after a short trial or after a change in a recent release.

1. Walk me through the last time you opened [app]. What were you trying to do, and what happened?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: Where did it feel slow, confusing, or not worth the effort?
2. When did you first think about deleting [app] or just stopping? What triggered that?
   Follow up: Was it a failed task, a notification, a login wall, storage or performance, or something else in your life?
3. After you stopped, what do you use instead, including a default phone app or doing the job in a browser?
   Follow up: What does that option get right that [app] did not?

## Questions: in-app subscription cancel

Use this group with people who cancelled a weekly, monthly, or annual plan, including those who kept the free app.

1. Walk me through the moment you cancelled the subscription. What had you just experienced in the app?
   Follow up: What did you think you would lose, and was that acceptable?
2. Which paid feature did you actually use, and which did you pay for but rarely open?
   Follow up: What would have made the plan feel like it earned the next renewal?
3. What almost made you stay, such as a different plan, a pause, or a lower price?
   Follow up: Did you look for those options before you cancelled?

## Questions: notifications, permissions, and effort

Use this group when notifications, permission prompts, or account setup may have driven the exit.

1. Tell me about the notifications or prompts you remember from [app]. Which ones helped, and which ones pushed you away?
   Follow up: What would a useful reminder look like for you?
2. Was there a permission, a login, or a setup step that felt like too much for what you wanted to do?
   Follow up: When did that appear, and what did you do?
3. If [app] were still on your home screen tomorrow, what would have to be different for you to open it again?
   Follow up: What should we leave as it is?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a consumer mobile app churn interview?

Ask about the last session, the moment they thought about deleting the app or cancelling a subscription, what they use instead, and whether notifications, permissions, or setup added too much effort. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit people who uninstalled my app?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite users from your own product, a list of cancelled subscribers, or your email list.

### Can people take the interview on their phone?

Yes. Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews. You send a link, and people can talk when they have time, including on a phone, without booking a call. Frank does not run video, WhatsApp, or chat interviews.
