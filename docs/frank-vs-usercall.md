# Frank vs Usercall: AI-Moderated Interviews Compared

**In short:** Frank and Usercall both run AI-moderated voice interviews: you set the questions, people talk with an AI interviewer when they have time, and you get transcripts and themes you can check against the original conversation. Choose Usercall if you need a recruited panel, or on-screen prototype testing; choose Frank if you want to interview customers you already have, starting from a free plan, with MCP included on every plan, including Free.

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai), a voice-first product built by Prelaunch Group, Inc. The product website is https://www.hifrank.ai (prelaunch.com is the parent company, not Frank's site). It runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. Usercall is a separate AI-moderated research platform. This page compares them from both companies' live public pages, opened on 16 November 2026. Prices and feature lists change, so check each site before you buy.

## When to choose which

**Choose Usercall** when you need people you do not already have. Usercall can recruit participants through panel partners, which it says reach 95M+ people in 151 countries, or you can bring your own. It is also the better pick when the interview itself has to show an image, mockup, or Figma prototype on screen (with screen recording), when you want to upload existing transcripts, survey files, tickets, or reviews for qualitative analysis, or when a researcher needs to listen in live and queue a question mid-interview (Core plan and above). Interview caps are also longer: up to 45 minutes on Pro, and up to one hour on request on any plan.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you do not need a panel. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), let many conversations run in parallel, and read the transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan with monthly voice minutes and no credit card, and MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth, so you can set up a study and read transcripts from those assistants. If you want many consistent voice conversations with your own customers, rather than a recruited study, that is the fit.

**Either tool can work** if you only need to share a link with people you already know, run voice interviews without a calendar, and read themes linked back to quotes. Usercall also lets you embed an interview on your site or in your product, and it documents a hosted MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. Those overlaps are why the choice usually comes down to recruiting, prototype testing, plan structure, and security documentation, not to whether both can run an AI-moderated voice interview.

For the non-agent workflow (set up a study, share a link or widget, read the themes), see [How to Automate Customer Interviews with AI](how-to-automate-customer-interviews-with-ai.md).

## How Frank works

You set a research goal and an interview guide. Frank suggests topics from that goal, then runs the voice interviews for you. You invite your own customers with a shareable interviewer link, or you embed a website widget so someone can start from your site. People join when they have time. There is no calendar to manage, and many interviews can run in parallel.

After each conversation you get a transcript. Answers are grouped into themes you can trace back to the original quotes. If you already work in an AI assistant, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) and ask it to help draft a study or read transcripts without leaving that chat. New research is created as a draft, so it is not published to participants until you approve it.

Frank interviews customers you can already reach. It does not find those people for you. If you have no customers yet, you will need another way to recruit. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews) in a cancellation flow, [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

## How Usercall works

On Usercall's site, you write an interview guide (Usercall can help draft it and suggest edits). You then either bring your own participants or ask Usercall to recruit. Recruited studies, Usercall says, usually return 15–30+ interviews in 48 to 72 hours, depending on the audience and the screener. You see a recruiting cost estimate before you commit; recruiting is quoted per study, with incentives included ([AI moderator](https://www.usercall.co/features/ai-moderator), [Recruit participants](https://www.usercall.co/recruit-participants), 2026-11-16).

Each interview is a live, one-to-one conversation with a real person. Usercall says participants are told they are talking to an AI. The moderator follows your guide, can probe vague answers (a per-question "Adaptive Follow-ups" setting), and can show an image, mockup, or Figma prototype on screen. People can join when it suits them. On Core and above, a researcher can listen in live and queue questions.

Usercall transcribes and codes interviews into themes linked to quotes. You can also upload other material, such as survey answers or support tickets, and ask questions about the data in a Q&A tab. Interviews can be started by a link, an embed, or a research trigger after a moment such as signup or cancellation. A hosted MCP connector is documented for Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor; a person still has to share the interview link or open a trigger activation URL ([Usercall API / MCP](https://app.usercall.co/docs/api#mcp-instructions), 2026-11-16).

## Pricing

Prices below are quoted as they appeared on each company's live pricing page on 16 November 2026. Prices change. Confirm the live page before you buy.

**Frank**, from [hifrank.ai/pricing](https://www.hifrank.ai/pricing):

- **Free:** $0. 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes per month (about 6 interviews at the 10-minute maximum), sentiment and thematic analysis. No credit card required.
- **Starter:** $59 per month, or $49 per month when billed yearly ($590/year). 120 voice minutes per month, 20-minute maximum interview length.
- **Growth:** $199 per month, or $166 per month when billed yearly ($1,990/year). 450 voice minutes per month, 20-minute maximum, with unused credits rolling over for 30 days.
- **Business:** $375 per month, or $312 per month when billed yearly ($3,750/year). 950 voice minutes per month, 30-minute maximum, plus everything in Growth.
- **Enterprise:** custom minute pools, SSO, API integration, and dedicated support; contact Frank.
- There is no free trial on paid plans; the Free plan is the way to try Frank. Cancel anytime: a paid plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, with no refund for the unused time. Students get 50% off after status is verified.

MCP is included on all Frank plans, including Free.

**Usercall**, from [usercall.co/pricing](https://www.usercall.co/pricing):

- **First interview:** free. Usercall says you can sign up and launch the first interview or analysis with no demo or sales call.
- **Lite:** $89 per month, or $899 yearly. 100 credits per month (about 10 to 20 voice interviews, depending on duration). Interviews up to 12 minutes, 2 interview links, 50+ languages, mockup/prototype testing, transcripts with summaries.
- **Core:** $199 per month, or $1,999 yearly. 300 credits per month (about 30 to 60 interviews). Interviews up to 25 minutes, 5 links, plus live observation, unlimited AI chat insight Q&A, branded links, and other Core features.
- **Pro:** $399 per month, or $3,999 yearly. 650 credits per month (about 65 to 130 interviews). Interviews up to 45 minutes, 10 links, up to 6 team seats, plus an outbound phone-calling add-on.
- **Custom:** contact Usercall for custom credits, seats, and interview length.
- Credits are shared between interviews and file analysis, and are used based on interview length and analysis depth. Any plan can run interviews up to one hour on request. Pay-as-you-go is available; Usercall says to contact them for details. Students and grant-funded researchers can ask about pricing before choosing a plan.

A like-for-like monthly cost depends on how long each interview is and whether you also pay for recruiting. Frank's published paid plans start lower than Usercall's Lite plan, and Frank offers a recurring Free tier. Usercall's first interview is free, and recruited participants are priced separately from the subscription. Neither comparison is complete until you add the length of your interviews and, if you need a panel, Usercall's recruiting estimate.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between Frank and Usercall?
Both run AI-moderated voice interviews and return transcripts and themes. The main difference is the job each one is set up to do. Usercall can recruit participants and run on-screen concept and prototype tests, and can also analyze uploaded files. Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you already have, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Frank does not recruit.

### When should I choose Usercall?
Choose Usercall when you need a recruited panel, when people must react to an image, mockup, or Figma prototype during the interview, when you want to upload existing qualitative files, or when a researcher needs to observe live and step in with a question (Core and above). Those capabilities are documented on Usercall's own site and are the cases where Usercall is the more specific fit.

### When should I choose Frank?
Choose Frank when you can already reach the customers you want to hear from and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a link or website widget, with many interviews running in parallel. Frank publishes a Free plan and includes MCP on every plan, including Free. It is a weaker fit if you need recruiting, on-screen prototype testing, or the longer interview caps Usercall lists.

### Does Frank recruit interview participants?
No. Frank does not recruit participants. Invite your own customers: people who recently signed up, cancelled, bought, or used the feature. If you have no customers yet, a recruiting service or your network is a better first step. Usercall can recruit through panel partners or work with people you bring.

### Do both tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Yes. Frank is available as an [MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. New Frank research is created as a draft and is not published to participants without your approval. Usercall documents a hosted MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor; a person still shares the interview link or activates a trigger. Usercall does not state on the pages reviewed whether MCP is included on every plan.

### How does Frank pricing compare with Usercall?
As of 16 November 2026, Frank's Free plan includes 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes per month with no credit card, and paid plans start at $59 per month, or $49 per month billed yearly. Usercall's first interview is free, and paid plans start at $89 per month, or $899 yearly, plus any recruiting fees. Prices change; check [Frank's pricing page](https://www.hifrank.ai/pricing) and [Usercall's pricing page](https://www.usercall.co/pricing) before you buy.

### How do Frank and Usercall handle security?
Frank's live security page states AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, with access controls you manage. Usercall's privacy policy is governed by the CCPA and, for EU data subjects, the GDPR, and a DPA is available on request. Usercall says data is encrypted in transit, access is controlled, and customer recordings, transcripts, and uploaded content are not used to train AI models. A SOC 2 claim was not stated on the Usercall pages reviewed, and usercall.co/security and usercall.co/trust were not live on 16 November 2026.
