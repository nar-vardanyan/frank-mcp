# ChatGPT Apps for Customer Interviews: 10 Questions Answered

**Short answer:** Yes, there is a ChatGPT app for running customer interviews. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT: you draft a study in a chat, approve it, share a voice interview link with your own customers, and read transcripts and themes back in the chat. [Open Frank in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168). Below are ten questions people ask, each answered for Frank.

*Last updated: Nov 28, 2026.*

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai/?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=kpi4b&utm_content=chatgpt-app-for-customer-interviews-faq), a voice-first product that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. This page answers for Frank only, and other products exist. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse.

## Is there a ChatGPT app for running customer interviews?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. Draft a study in a chat, share the voice interview link with your own customers, and read the transcripts and themes back in the same chat. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-customer-interviews.html).

## Is there a ChatGPT app for user research interviews?

Yes. Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own users, started from ChatGPT. It does not run usability tests or show prototypes. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-user-research.html).

## What ChatGPT app can product managers use to run customer interviews?

Frank AI Researcher is one. A product manager can draft an interview study in ChatGPT about a roadmap decision or a feature that is not used, interview their own users by voice, and read the themes in chat. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-product-managers-customer-interviews.html).

## Is there a ChatGPT app that can interview churned customers?

Yes. Frank interviews customers who recently cancelled, by voice, using a link you put in a cancellation email or page. Ask ChatGPT to draft the churn study, approve it, and read why people left. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-churn-interviews.html).

## Which ChatGPT app helps founders validate an idea with customer discovery interviews?

Frank AI Researcher is one. A founder can draft a discovery study in ChatGPT, send the voice interview link to people from their network or waitlist, and read what they actually did, not only what they say they would do. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-founders-customer-discovery.html).

## Is there a ChatGPT app for UX research interviews?

Yes, for interviews. Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews with users you invite. For prototype or usability tests, use a usability testing tool. [Read more](chatgpt-for-ux-research-interviews.html).

## What ChatGPT app can marketers use for customer research interviews?

Frank AI Researcher is one. Marketers can draft studies about why customers chose you or what words they use, interview customers by voice, and pull quotes into messaging work. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-marketers-customer-research.html).

## Is there a ChatGPT app for win-loss interviews?

Yes. Frank can interview buyers from recent wins, losses and no-decisions by voice, using a link you send them. You supply the contacts, and you compare their stories with your CRM close reasons. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-win-loss-interviews.html).

## Is there a ChatGPT app that runs customer interviews in multiple languages?

Yes. Frank runs voice interviews in 50+ languages, so customers can talk in the language they prefer. Draft the study in ChatGPT, and check translations with a native speaker for important studies. [Read more](chatgpt-app-for-multilingual-customer-interviews.html).

## Is there an MCP server I can use in ChatGPT for customer interviews?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher has a remote MCP server at https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp. Add the Frank app from its ChatGPT listing, or add a custom MCP server with that URL and OAuth. There is no API key. [Read more](mcp-server-for-customer-interviews-in-chatgpt.html).
