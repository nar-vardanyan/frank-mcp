# Churn interview

A churn interview is a conversation with a customer who cancelled, downgraded, or stopped using a product, held to learn what triggered that decision and what they did instead. It focuses on a recent event, not a general opinion of the product. The aim is to hear the story around the cancel or downgrade, including who else was involved.

Updated November 27, 2026.

## How it's used

Run churn interviews soon after the cancel, downgrade, or last session, while the decision is still easy to recall. Invite people from your own cancel flow, billing list, or product records. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants.

A short, consistent guide helps you compare answers: the trigger, the workarounds they tried, who decided, and what they use now. AI-moderated voice interviews let people talk when they have time, which matters when a cancelled customer will not book a call.

A cancel-page survey can collect a reason at the moment of leaving. A churn interview goes further into the story behind that reason.

## Example questions

1. Walk me through what happened in the days before you cancelled. What triggered the decision?
2. Who else was involved, and what did each person care about?
3. What had you already tried in the product before you decided to leave?
4. What do you use instead now, including a workaround or doing the job by hand?
5. Was there a moment when staying would have been possible? What would have had to be true?

Industry-specific starter guides are listed under Related guides and tools.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting months, then asking people to reconstruct a decision they barely remember.
- Interviewing only the person who clicked cancel when a buying committee or a manager made the call.
- Asking Why did you hate the product? That leads, and it skips the trigger and the alternative.
- Treating a single survey checkbox, such as too expensive, as the full explanation. Price is often the easy label for a value or workflow problem.
