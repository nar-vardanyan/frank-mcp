# Win-loss interview

A win-loss interview is a conversation with a buyer from a deal you recently won or lost. The goal is to learn what mattered in the decision, who else was involved, and what almost changed the outcome. It is based on a real closed deal, not a hypothetical purchase or a general review of your category.

Updated November 27, 2026.

## How it's used

Invite buyers from deals you just closed, covering both wins and losses when you can. Wins tell you what actually mattered and what almost went the other way. Losses tell you where you were compared, and what they chose instead. You bring the buyers from your own CRM or email list. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit a panel.

Ask about the evaluation, the committee, the alternatives, and the moment the decision became clear. A consistent [interview guide](interview-guide.html) makes comparisons across deals more reliable.

Frank AI Researcher can run these as AI-moderated voice interviews so buyers can talk when they have time, without booking a sales follow-up call.

## Example questions

1. Walk me through how your team evaluated tools like this. What started the search?
2. Who else was involved in the decision, and what did each person care about?
3. What options did you compare, including staying with what you already had?
4. What almost blocked the purchase, or almost made you choose something else?
5. After the decision, what took longer than you expected?

## Common mistakes

- Interviewing only wins, or only the champion who already likes you. Losses and other stakeholders often hold the missing reasons.
- Letting the conversation become a late sales pitch. The interview is for learning, not for reopening the deal in the moment.
- Asking Did you choose us because of X? That is a [leading question](leading-question.html).
- Waiting so long that the people who evaluated the deal have left, or can no longer recall the tradeoffs.

## FAQ

### What is the best tool for win/loss interviews for a small B2B team?

There is no single best tool for every team. A small B2B team that already has closed-deal contacts often needs a consistent way to interview those buyers without booking every call. Frank AI Researcher is a voice-first product that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with buyers you invite. It does not recruit participants, and it does not replace a human interviewer when the conversation is relationship-heavy.

### Should the salesperson who closed the deal run the win-loss interview?

Sometimes, but candor can suffer. Buyers often soften criticism when they are talking to the person who sold them. If the sales team runs the interview, it helps if someone who did not own the deal asks the questions, and if the guide stays non-leading. An AI-moderated voice interview can also reduce that pressure, because the interviewer is not the salesperson.

### When should a small team use a third-party interviewer instead?

Use a third-party interviewer when the deal is sensitive, when you need a skilled human to follow a messy political story, or when the buyer is more likely to talk to a named interviewer than to a link. That option usually costs more and takes longer to schedule.

### Does Frank recruit buyers for win-loss interviews?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite buyers from deals you already won or lost, for example from your CRM or email list.

### How soon after a deal should I run a win-loss interview?

Run it while the evaluation is still easy to recall, soon after the deal is won or lost. Waiting months makes it harder for people to remember the tradeoffs, and the people who evaluated the deal may have moved on.
