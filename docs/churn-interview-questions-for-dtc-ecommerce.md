## When to use this guide

Use these question groups after a subscription cancel or skip, a customer who bought once and did not return, or a return that ended the relationship. Invite people from your own order, subscription, or email list. Frank does not recruit participants. Replace [product] and [brand] with the names they use. For interviews about a recent purchase with active buyers, see [customer interview questions for DTC ecommerce](customer-interview-questions-for-dtc-ecommerce.html).

## Questions: subscription cancel or skip

Use this group with subscribers who cancelled, paused, or skipped more than once and then left.

1. Walk me through the moment you decided to cancel or pause. What had you just received, or just not received?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: Was it the product, the timing, the price, or the feeling that you already had enough?
2. What almost made you stay, such as a skip, a swap, a pause, or a different plan?
   Follow up: Did you look for that option, and was it easy to find?
3. After you cancelled, what did you replace the subscription with, including buying the same thing only when you need it?
   Follow up: What would bring you back for a single box or a new plan?

## Questions: one order and no return

Use this group when first-order customers do not place a second order, even if they did not formally cancel anything.

1. Walk me through your last order from [brand], from the moment you decided to buy to the moment you used the product.
   Follow up: What felt good, and where did it disappoint?
2. What would have needed to be true for you to order again?
   Follow up: Was it the product itself, the fit, the price, shipping, or the after-purchase messages?
3. What did you buy instead the next time you needed something in this category?
   Follow up: What did that option get right that we did not?

## Questions: returns, delivery, and the last impression

Use this group when a return, a late delivery, or a checkout or packaging issue is part of why they left.

1. Tell me about the return, exchange, or delivery problem, if you had one. What did you need, and what happened?
   Follow up: How did that experience change whether you would buy again?
2. After the order arrived, what was different from what the page, the ad, or the email had led you to expect?
   Follow up: Which detail should have been clearer before you paid?
3. If a friend were about to buy from [brand] for the first time, what would you tell them, and what would you tell us to fix first?
   Follow up: Is there a version of this product or this plan you would still want?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a DTC ecommerce churn interview?

Ask about the cancel or skip moment, why a first order did not lead to a second, and whether a return, delivery issue, or expectation gap played a part. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide, and stay with the last order or the cancel event.

### Does Frank recruit shoppers who cancelled or stopped buying?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite people from your own order, subscription, or email list.

### Should I interview skipped subscribers the same way as cancelled ones?

You can use the same subscription group for both, then listen for whether they wanted a pause, a swap, or a true stop. If they only skipped, ask what would make them receive the next box. If they cancelled, ask what they replaced it with.
