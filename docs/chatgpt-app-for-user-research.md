## What kind of user research it covers

Frank runs interviews, the part of user research that explains why people do what they do. That includes discovery interviews about a problem someone already has, onboarding interviews with new users, interviews about a feature people saw but did not use, churn interviews, and jobs-to-be-done switch interviews.

It is not a tool for every method. Interviews explain why. Use a survey when you need to know how many people feel a certain way, and a usability test when you need to watch someone try a task.

## A user research study from ChatGPT

1. **Start with the research question.** Tell ChatGPT what you need to learn and which decision it will inform.
2. **Let ChatGPT draft the study in Frank.** A study created through MCP stays a draft until you approve publishing.
3. **Review it like a moderator would.** Read the guide, take the interview yourself, and remove leading or stacked questions.
4. **Publish and share.** Send the interviewer link to users by email or in your product, or embed a website widget. People talk with the AI interviewer by voice when they have time, without booking a call.
5. **Synthesize in chat.** Ask ChatGPT for themes with exact quotes, then open a few full transcripts and check the quotes in context.

## Prompts to start with

**Generative interview about a workflow**

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to understand how [user type] handle [task] today. Write 6 to 8 open questions about the last time they did it: what started it, the steps they took, the tools and people involved, and where it slowed down. Do not mention our product's features. Wait for me to review the guide.
```

**Read the results**

```text
From Frank, list the themes in my [study name] study with two exact quotes each, and note which themes came from only one interview.
```

## Where it fits next to other research methods

| Method | What it answers | Where Frank fits |
|---|---|---|
| Interview | Why people act the way they do, in their own words | Frank runs these by voice with users you invite |
| Usability test | Where people get stuck on a specific task | Not Frank. Frank does not run usability tests |
| Survey | How many people feel or do something | Not Frank. Use a survey tool, then interview to explain the numbers |
| Product analytics | What happened in the product | Not Frank. Use analytics to choose who to interview |

## What it does not do

- **No participant recruiting or panel.** You invite users you already have.
- **No usability tests, prototype tests, or image tests.** Frank does not show designs or prototypes.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **No research repository.** Frank collects new interviews. It is not a place to store calls, tickets, and reviews from other sources.

## FAQ

### Is there a ChatGPT app for user research?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. It covers user research interviews: ChatGPT drafts the study, Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews with users you invite, and you read the transcripts and themes in the chat.

### Can ChatGPT do user research on its own?

ChatGPT can help you plan a study, write questions, and analyze transcripts you paste in. It does not talk to your users by itself. With Frank connected, the interviews run by voice and the transcripts come back to the chat.

### Can Frank run usability tests in ChatGPT?

No. Frank does not run usability tests or show designs, prototypes, or images. It runs voice interviews, which you can use before or after a usability test to hear how people use your live product.

### Does Frank recruit users to interview?

No. Frank does not recruit participants. You invite your own users by link, email, in-app message, or website widget.

### Can I interview users in other languages?

Yes. Frank supports multilingual interviews. Have a native speaker check the guide and the key quotes.
