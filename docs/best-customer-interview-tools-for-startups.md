## What startups actually need from an interview tool

Startup research has a different shape from enterprise research. Budgets are small, the team is busy, and the audience is usually early users, trial sign-ups or a waitlist rather than a recruited panel. What matters most:

- **A real free tier or a cheap first study.** You should be able to test the method before paying.
- **No scheduling.** Founders lose whole days to calendar back-and-forth.
- **Your own users.** Built-in panels add cost you may not need yet.
- **Transcripts you can trust.** Themes are only useful if you can check them against quotes.
- **Speed.** Answers this week, not next quarter.

You also don't need hundreds of interviews to learn something. For a fairly similar group and a focused question, one widely cited study found themes largely settled within the first 12 interviews ([Guest, Bunce & Johnson, 2006](https://www.sfu.ca/~palys/GuestEtAl-2006-HowManyInterviewsAreEnough.pdf)).

## How to choose as a startup

1. **No users yet?** Start with recruiting: your network, communities, or a tool with a panel. An AI interviewer doesn't solve an empty audience.
2. **Have users, short on time?** Use an AI-moderated tool so interviews happen without calls. Compare free tiers first.
3. **Want to do every call yourself?** A scheduling-and-repository tool is a better fit than an AI interviewer.
4. **Budget is the constraint?** Compare the cost of the study you'll actually run: minutes per interview, number of interviews, and any recruiting fees.

Whichever you pick, write the decision first, keep the guide to 6 to 8 open questions about recent behavior ([Nielsen Norman Group, Open-Ended vs. Closed Questions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/open-ended-questions/)), and check every theme against quotes before acting.

## When AI interviews are a poor fit

Nielsen Norman Group found AI interviewers work best as a complement to human moderation, and are weaker for messy discovery, deep domain topics or anything someone needs to show on screen ([AI-Moderated Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/)). In the earliest days, when you are still finding the problem, do the first conversations yourself.
