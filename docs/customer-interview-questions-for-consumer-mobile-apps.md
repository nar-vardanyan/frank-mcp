## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand the first session, why someone came back or did not, or why they deleted the app. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [app] with the name users use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite users from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you opened [app]. What were you trying to get done?
2. Walk me through your first session after install. Where did you get stuck or lose interest?
3. What brought you back on a later day, or what kept you from coming back?
4. Tell me about a notification or a prompt that you found useful, or one that made you want to leave.
5. What almost made you delete the app, or already made you delete a similar one?
6. When do you use this app instead of a website, another app, or doing nothing?
7. How does it fit into your day, and when does it get in the way?
8. If you could change one thing about the first week with this app, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a consumer mobile app interview?

Ask about a recent session: what they were trying to get done, where the first session stalled, what brought them back or kept them away, and what almost made them delete the app. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit mobile app users?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite users from your own product or email list.

### When should I use this first-session and retention guide?

Use it when you want to change the first session, return visits, notifications, or the moment someone considers deleting the app. Cut or rewrite questions that do not serve that decision, and keep a consistent core so you can compare answers.
