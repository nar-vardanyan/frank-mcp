## Why interview churned customers, not only survey them

A cancellation survey is useful for counting categories such as price or a missing feature. It usually cannot tell you which moment, which alternative, or which feature was behind that category. A short voice interview asks for the story: what changed, what they tried, and what they use now. Use the survey to size reasons and the interviews to understand them.

## Where to put the interview link

- On the cancellation confirmation page.
- In the cancellation confirmation email, or a follow-up a few days later.
- In a website widget on your cancellation page.

Ask soon after the cancel, while the decision is easy to recall. Keep the invite short, say why you are asking, and make clear it is not a sales call. Cancelled customers rarely book a meeting, so a link they can use when they have time asks less of them. Keep live calls for large accounts. Frank does not contact customers for you; the invite comes from your own product or email.

## What to listen for

- **The trigger:** what changed.
- **The struggle:** what they were trying to get done.
- **The alternative:** what they use now, including a spreadsheet or nothing.
- **The save point:** what, if anything, would have kept them, and when.

Stop interviewing when new conversations stop adding triggers.

## Prompts for churn interviews

**Draft the churn study**

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to interview customers who cancelled in the last 30 days so I can decide what to fix first. Write 6 to 8 open questions about the recent decision: what they hoped for, when they first thought about leaving, what they tried, who else was involved, what they use now, and what would have changed their mind. Do not open with "Why did you leave?" Wait for me to review the guide.
```

**Read the results by trigger**

```text
From Frank, group the interviews in my churn study by trigger. For each trigger, list the interviews, the plan or tenure if it is in the transcript, and two exact quotes. Mark triggers backed by a single interview as low confidence.
```

## What it does not do

- **No contacting customers.** Frank does not recruit participants or email your cancelled customers. You share the link.
- **No win-back offers.** The interview is for learning, so keep discounts and save attempts out of the guide.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **No automatic publishing.** A churn study drafted in ChatGPT stays a draft until you approve it.
