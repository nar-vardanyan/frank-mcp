## When to choose which

**Choose Marvin** when the gap is storing, tagging, and analyzing research you already have. Marvin's pricing page describes a research repository and analysis platform. AI-moderated interviews are listed as part of an AI Research Suite add-on for paid plans, next to live intercepts. A free plan is offered. Paid tiers on the page are Starter, Pro, and Enterprise, with paid prices behind "Book a demo" or "Contact us." No public dollar prices for those paid plans were on the pricing page opened 20 November 2026. A built-in recruitment panel is not documented on that page; the page mentions a research panel for managing contacts. Marvin MCP is listed in the feature comparison. Plan-by-plan inclusion is not documented in the static page text.

**Choose Frank** when the gap is running new interviews with customers you already have. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=marvin-alternatives) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=marvin-alternatives). Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews and returns transcripts and themes. It supports multilingual interviews, publishes a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month, no credit card) and paid plans from $49/month billed yearly, and is SOC 2 and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=marvin-alternatives). MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.

Frank is not a repository for calls, tickets, and reviews. Many teams collect with Frank and organize elsewhere. Other repositories are compared on [Dovetail alternatives](dovetail-alternatives.md).

## FAQ

### Is Frank a Marvin alternative?

It depends on the job. Frank AI Researcher collects new AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers and returns transcripts and themes. Marvin is a research repository for organizing and analyzing research data. If your gap is running interviews, Frank is built for that. If your gap is storing and tagging research you already have, Marvin is built for that.

### Does Marvin run AI-moderated interviews?

Marvin's pricing page lists AI-moderated interviews as part of an AI Research Suite add-on available for paid plans. Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews on every plan, including Free.

### Does Marvin publish paid prices?

The pricing page offers a free plan and shows Starter, Pro, and Enterprise, but paid prices are behind "Book a demo" or "Contact us." As of 20 November 2026, no public dollar prices were listed. Frank publishes a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month) and paid plans starting at $49/month billed yearly.

### Is MCP included on Frank's Free plan?

Yes. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.
