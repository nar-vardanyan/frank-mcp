## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when a workflow spans admins, managers, and employees, and you need to understand a rollout, a daily task, or a switch. Interview one group at a time with the same core questions, and note which role each person has. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite people from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [product] for a real HR task, such as hiring, onboarding, payroll, or a review. What were you trying to get done?
2. Walk me through who does which steps: admin, manager, and employee.
3. Where does the workflow break, or get handed off badly, between those groups?
4. What did you use before this, and what do you still do in a spreadsheet or another tool?
5. Tell me about a time implementation, a data load, or a switch caused a problem.
6. What almost made you stop using it, or use it only for some teams or tasks?
7. What would need to be true for employees or managers to use it the way you hoped?
8. If you could change one step in the workflow, which step and why?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in an HR tech customer interview?

Ask about a recent HR task: who does each step, where the handoff between admins, managers, and employees breaks, what went wrong in implementation or a switch, and which step they would change. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit HR admins, managers, or employees?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite admins, managers, or employees from your own product or email list.

### Should I interview admins and employees with the same guide?

Use the same core questions so you can compare answers, and interview one group at a time. Note which role each person has, and follow up on the steps that group actually performs.
