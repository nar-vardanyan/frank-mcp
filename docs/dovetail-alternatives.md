## Why are you looking?

People leave an analysis platform for three different reasons, and each points to a different tool:

- **Cost.** Dovetail's pricing page, opened 20 Nov 2026, shows Bronze at $4,800 a year for the platform, plus credits at 15c each, and a 30-day free trial on Gold ([Dovetail pricing](https://dovetail.com/pricing/)). A "5 users" seat count is not documented on that page as of Nov 2026. If that is more than your research volume justifies, you want a cheaper repository.
- **Scope.** Dovetail describes an AI-native customer intelligence platform that ingests calls, tickets, surveys, reviews and research ([Dovetail](https://dovetail.com/pricing/)). If you only need to analyze interviews, a narrower tool may do.
- **Missing input.** Sometimes the problem is not analysis at all: you have too few interviews to analyze. Then a collection tool helps more than another repository.

## How to choose

1. **Price is the problem?** Compare Condens Lite, Looppanel Solo and Marvin's free plan against your real number of projects a year.
2. **You want a broader suite?** Great Question bundles recruiting your own customers with research tools; check which features are Enterprise-only.
3. **You need more interviews, not more storage?** Add Frank to collect voice interviews from customers you can invite, and keep your repository.
4. **Your security review matters?** Ask each vendor for its current certifications. Dovetail's certification list is not documented in the pricing-page text checked for price on 20 Nov 2026.

## Switching without losing your data

1. **Export before you cancel.** Check each tool's export options; export formats vary and we have not compared them.
2. **Run one project in the new tool** before you move the archive.
3. **Keep one source of truth** for themes. Whichever repository you pick, every theme should link back to an exact quote.
4. **Add collection when you need more interviews.** If your backlog of unanalyzed interviews is the problem, a repository helps; if the problem is too few interviews, add Frank.

## Limits of AI in analysis and interviews

Nielsen Norman Group found AI interviewers work best as a complement to human moderation, not a replacement, and are weaker for messy problem spaces and deep domain topics ([AI-Moderated Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/)). AI summaries in any repository can over-generalize, so check them against the source.
