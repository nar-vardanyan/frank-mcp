## First: why are you looking?

People leave an analysis platform for three different reasons, and each points to a different tool:

- **Cost.** Dovetail's published Bronze plan is $4,800 a year for the platform and 5 users, plus credits at 15c each; a free 30-day trial covers Gold ([Dovetail pricing](https://dovetail.com/pricing/)). If that is more than your research volume justifies, you want a cheaper repository.
- **Scope.** Dovetail describes an AI-native customer intelligence platform that ingests calls, tickets, surveys, reviews and research ([Dovetail](https://dovetail.com/pricing/)). If you only need to analyze interviews, a narrower tool may do.
- **Missing input.** Sometimes the problem is not analysis at all: you have too few interviews to analyze. Then a collection tool helps more than another repository.

## The repositories

### Condens

Condens is a research repository and AI analysis platform. Lite is from €15 per month; Business is from €500 per month paid yearly with 5 contributors included, AI search and analysis, and a ChatGPT and Claude connection ([Condens pricing](https://condens.io/pricing/)). It fits teams that want a repository at a lower starting price.

### Marvin

Marvin pairs a repository with an AI notetaker, reports and highlight reels, and thematic and sentiment analysis on Pro. Its AI Research Suite add-on, available for all paid plans, lists live intercepts and AI-moderated interviews ([Marvin pricing](https://heymarvin.com/pricing)). It has a free plan to start. It fits teams that want analysis and some collection in one vendor.

### Looppanel

Looppanel positions itself as an AI research assistant that auto-tags, searches and analyzes interviews and surveys, with transcription across 96+ languages and a Looppanel MCP to query research from Claude ([Looppanel](https://www.looppanel.com/)). Its Solo plan is listed at $49 per month with a 15-day free trial ([Pricing](https://www.looppanel.com/pricing)). It fits solo researchers and small teams.

### Great Question

Great Question is a broader research platform; its pricing FAQ says recruiting your own customers is free, with external recruitment and incentives charged per participant. Self-serve is $129 per seat per month, and its pricing FAQ lists AI-moderated interviews, the Great Question MCP and the User Interviews panel as Enterprise features ([Pricing](https://www.greatquestion.com/pricing)). It fits teams that want a broader suite and can wait for Enterprise to add AI-moderated interviews.

## How to choose

1. **Price is the problem?** Compare Condens Lite, Looppanel Solo and Marvin's free plan against your real number of projects a year.
2. **You want a broader suite?** Great Question bundles recruiting your own customers with research tools; check which features are Enterprise-only.
3. **You need more interviews, not more storage?** Add Frank to collect voice interviews from customers you can invite, and keep your repository.
4. **Your security review matters?** Ask each vendor for its current certifications; Dovetail lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and GDPR on its pricing page ([Dovetail](https://dovetail.com/pricing/)).

## Switching without losing your data

1. **Export first.** Check each tool's export options before you cancel; export formats vary and we have not compared them.
2. **Run one project in the new tool** before you move the archive.
3. **Keep one source of truth** for themes. Whichever repository you pick, every theme should link back to an exact quote.
4. **Add collection only if you need it.** If your backlog of unanalyzed interviews is the problem, a repository helps; if the problem is too few interviews, add Frank.

## Limits of AI in analysis and interviews

Nielsen Norman Group found AI interviewers work best as a complement to human moderation, not a replacement, and are weaker for messy problem spaces and deep domain topics ([AI-Moderated Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/)). AI summaries in any repository can over-generalize, so check them against the source.
