## What a churn interview tool must do

Churn interviews are different from other research. The people you need just left, owe you nothing, and won't book a call. A good tool:

- **Starts from a link.** On the cancellation confirmation page or in the confirmation email, while memory is fresh.
- **Needs no scheduling.** AI-moderated interviews let people take part when it suits them, without a human facilitator ([Nielsen Norman Group, AI-Moderated Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/)).
- **Asks follow-ups.** "Too expensive" means nothing until you ask what changed. Open questions surface reasons a dropdown can't ([Nielsen Norman Group, Open-Ended vs. Closed Questions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/open-ended-questions/)).
- **Focuses on the moment.** The critical incident technique asks people to recall a specific event that affected an outcome ([Nielsen Norman Group, Critical Incident Technique](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/critical-incident-technique/)); for churn, that's the moment they started doubting the product.
- **Uses your own customers.** You need people who actually cancelled, so recruiting panels matter less here.

## How to run a churn interview program

1. **Pick the decision.** For example, which onboarding step to rebuild, or whether the entry plan is priced wrong.
2. **Write 5 to 7 questions** about what happened: when they signed up, when doubt started, what they tried, what they use now, what would have kept them.
3. **Place the invite in the cancel flow** and in the confirmation email.
4. **Read transcripts weekly.** Group stories by trigger and segment, and check every theme against quotes.
5. **Size what you found** with your cancellation survey and billing data. Interviews explain why; counts come from data ([Nielsen Norman Group, Should You Run a Survey?](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/should-you-run-a-survey/)).

Themes often settle within about a dozen interviews for a fairly similar group ([Guest, Bunce & Johnson, 2006](https://www.sfu.ca/~palys/GuestEtAl-2006-HowManyInterviewsAreEnough.pdf)), so start small and keep it running.

## When a tool is the wrong answer

If only a few customers cancel each month, call them yourself. For high-value accounts, a relationship conversation is worth a human. And Nielsen Norman Group found AI interviewers work best as a complement to human moderation ([AI-Moderated Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-interviewers/)).
