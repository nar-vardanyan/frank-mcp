# Is there a ChatGPT app for customer interviews?

**Yes. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. From a chat, you can draft a customer interview study, share the voice interview link with your own customers, and read the transcripts and themes when the interviews are done. Frank runs the AI-moderated voice interviews.** [Open Frank in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168).

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai/?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=chatgpt-app-answer), a voice-first software product that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. Frank does not recruit participants. This page answers the question for Frank only.

## Install Frank in ChatGPT in 3 steps

1. **Open the listing.** Go to the [Frank AI Researcher listing in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168) and add Frank.
2. **Sign in and approve access.** Sign in to Frank in your browser when asked and approve access. There is no API key.
3. **Use it in a chat.** Type `@`, select Frank, and ask: "List my Frank studies."

If the app is not available in your workspace, use the custom MCP server path in ChatGPT on the web: go to **Plugins**, select the plus button, choose **Add custom MCP server**, name it Frank AI Researcher, set the server URL to `https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp`, choose OAuth, then create and install the plugin and complete sign-in. OpenAI documents this path in [Add custom MCP server](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server). Workspace permissions apply.

## What you can do from a chat

- **Draft a study.** Describe what you want to learn and who you want to hear from. ChatGPT creates the study in Frank. A study created through MCP stays a draft until you approve publishing, so nothing reaches participants before that step.
- **Review and publish.** Read the guide, take the interview yourself, fix anything confusing or leading, then approve publishing.
- **Share the link with your own customers.** Send the [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=chatgpt-app-answer) by email or in your product, or embed a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=chatgpt-app-answer). People talk with the AI interviewer by voice when they have time, without booking a call.
- **Read transcripts and themes in chat.** Ask ChatGPT to list studies, read transcripts, and summarize themes with quotes. Then open a few full transcripts and check the quotes you plan to act on.

## Example prompts

**Check the connection**

```text
@Frank list my Frank studies.
```

**Draft a churn study**

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to interview customers who cancelled in the last 30 days. Keep the interview under 10 minutes. Write 6 to 8 open questions about the recent decision: what they hoped for, when they first thought about leaving, what they tried, what they use now, and what would have changed their mind. Wait for me to review the guide.
```

**Draft a discovery study**

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study about how [customer type] handle [problem] today. Ask about the last time it happened, what they did, and what it cost them. Do not mention my idea. Keep it as a draft until I approve it.
```

**Read the results**

```text
From Frank, summarize the main themes in my [study name] study, with two exact quotes for each theme. List anything that does not fit the main themes.
```

## Who it is for

Frank in ChatGPT fits teams that already work in ChatGPT and want to hear from customers or users they can already reach:

- Founders checking a problem, churn, or product-market fit.
- Product managers deciding what to build next or why a feature goes unused.
- UX researchers who want interview context around a workflow.
- Customer success and marketing teams who want customers' own words about renewals, onboarding, or why they bought.

Frank is voice-first, supports multilingual interviews, and is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant.

## What it does not do

- **No participant recruiting.** Frank does not recruit participants or provide a panel. You invite people you already have.
- **Voice interviews only.** Frank does not run video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **No prototype or image tests.** Frank does not show designs, prototypes, or images, and it does not run usability tests.
- **No automatic publishing.** A study drafted from ChatGPT stays a draft until you approve it.
- **Not a replacement for judgment.** AI summaries can over-generalize. Check themes against the quotes, and use a survey or product data when you need to know how many people feel a certain way.

## FAQ

### Is there a ChatGPT app for customer interviews?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. You can draft a customer interview study from a chat, share the voice interview link with your own customers, and read the transcripts and themes in the same chat. The listing is at [https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168).

### Can ChatGPT run user interviews?

Not by itself. On its own, ChatGPT can help you write questions and analyze transcripts you paste in. With the Frank app connected, ChatGPT can draft a study in Frank, and Frank runs AI-moderated voice interviews with the people you invite. The study stays a draft until you approve publishing.

### How do I install Frank in ChatGPT?

Open the Frank listing in ChatGPT and add Frank, sign in to Frank in your browser and approve access, then type @ in a chat, select Frank, and ask it to list your Frank studies. There is no API key. If the app is not available in your workspace, add a custom MCP server in ChatGPT on the web with the URL https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp and choose OAuth.

### Does Frank work in Claude or Grok too?

Yes. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse. In other MCP clients, connect to https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp.

### Is it free?

There is a Free plan with 60 voice minutes a month and no card required. MCP, which is how ChatGPT connects to Frank, is included on every plan, including Free.

### Does Frank recruit participants?

No. Frank does not recruit participants. You share the interviewer link or a website widget with customers or users you already have.
