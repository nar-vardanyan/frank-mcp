## When to choose which

**Choose GetWhy** when you want AI-moderated video interviews and a recruited panel. GetWhy's platform page says it can run video interviews in 100+ languages and describes access to 300M high-quality participants. Public prices are not documented. On 20 November 2026, getwhy.io/pricing returned 404, and the homepage and platform calls to action are "Book a demo." Whether you can bring your own customer list is not documented on the pages opened. MCP is not documented on those pages.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you want voice interviews. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), conversations can run in parallel, and you read transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month, no credit card) and paid plans from $49/month billed yearly. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. Frank supports multilingual interviews.

## FAQ

### Is Frank a GetWhy alternative for interviewing my own customers?

Yes, if you already have customers to invite. Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers from a shareable link or website widget and returns transcripts and themes. It has a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month) and MCP on every plan, including Free.

### Does GetWhy recruit participants?

Yes, according to GetWhy's platform page, which states access to 300M high-quality participants. Frank does not recruit participants.

### Does GetWhy publish prices?

Not on a public pricing page. As of 20 November 2026, getwhy.io/pricing returned 404, and the site invites buyers to book a demo. Frank publishes a Free plan and paid plans starting at $49/month billed yearly.

### Is MCP included on Frank's Free plan?

Yes. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.
