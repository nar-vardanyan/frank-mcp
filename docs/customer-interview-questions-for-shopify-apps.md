## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you have a concrete decision, such as improving the first session after install or reducing uninstalls. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [app] with the name merchants use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite merchants from your own install, uninstall, or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you installed [app], or an app like it, in your Shopify store. What were you trying to get done?
2. What almost stopped you from installing it?
3. Walk me through the first time you used it after install. Where did you get stuck?
4. Tell me about a time you uninstalled an app, or came close to uninstalling this one. What triggered that?
5. What had changed between the day you installed it and the day you considered removing it?
6. What, if anything, would have made you keep it?
7. How do you decide whether an app is worth the monthly charge, and who else is involved?
8. If you could change one thing about how this app fits into running your store, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask Shopify merchants in a customer interview?

Ask about a recent install or uninstall: what they were trying to get done, what almost stopped the install, where they got stuck after install, what triggered a removal, and what would have made them keep the app. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit Shopify merchants?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite merchants from your own install, uninstall, or email list.

### When should I use this install and uninstall guide?

Use it when you want to change onboarding after install, the moment a merchant considers uninstalling, or how you explain the monthly charge. Cut or rewrite questions that do not serve that decision, and keep a consistent core so you can compare answers.
