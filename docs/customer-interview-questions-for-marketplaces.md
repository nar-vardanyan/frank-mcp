## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand a match, a missed match, or why volume moves off the marketplace. Interview buyers and sellers separately with the same core questions, and note which side each person is on. Replace [marketplace] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite people from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [marketplace] as a buyer or as a seller. What were you trying to get done?
2. How did you decide to participate on that side, and what almost stopped you?
3. Walk me through a recent successful transaction. Where did it feel easy, and where did it stall?
4. Tell me about a time you could not find a match: no supply, no demand, or a mismatch on price, timing, or quality.
5. What do you do when the other side is slow, unreliable, or missing?
6. What would make you bring more volume to this marketplace, or take volume elsewhere?
7. How do the rules, fees, or trust tools affect whether you stay?
8. If you could change one thing to improve the other side of the marketplace, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a marketplace customer interview?

Ask about a recent transaction or a missed match: what they were trying to get done, where supply or demand was missing, what they do when the other side is unreliable, and what would make them bring more volume or take it elsewhere. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit buyers or sellers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite buyers and sellers from your own product or email list.

### Should I interview both sides of the marketplace?

Yes, when the decision depends on supply and demand together. Interview buyers and sellers separately with the same core questions, and note which side each person is on so you can compare where the match breaks.
