## What Conveo is, and why people look elsewhere

Conveo describes itself as a fully AI-powered video interview platform covering study design, recruitment, interviews and analysis, with "AI-led, video-first asynchronous interviews across languages, globally, 24/7" ([Conveo, Product](https://conveo.ai/product)). It recruits through CSV lists, external panels, QR codes and WhatsApp invites, and its product-page FAQ describes integrated recruitment via Respondent.io, typically a $6 recruitment fee per participant plus incentives of usually $1 per interview minute (about $21 for a 15-minute interview). You can also use your own panel or recruiter with a respondent link ([Conveo, Product FAQ](https://conveo.ai/product)). The page lists GDPR, SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022, plus zero AI model training on client data (same source). Its site offers "Start for free" and "Book a demo" ([conveo.ai](https://conveo.ai/)); a full price list was not published on the pages reviewed.

Teams usually look for an alternative for one of four reasons:

- **They don't need video.** Many customer conversations, especially B2B, work fine by voice, and some participants prefer not to be on camera.
- **They want a published price.** A self-serve plan is easier to start than a sales process.
- **They already have customers to talk to.** Built-in recruiting matters less when your users are the audience.
- **They want to work from an AI assistant.** An MCP server lets you run studies from ChatGPT, Claude and similar tools.

## The alternatives, one by one

### 1. Frank: voice interviews with your own customers

Frank is voice-first. You create a study, share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or embed a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), and customers talk with the AI interviewer when they have time. Every interview gets a transcript, and answers are grouped into themes you can check against the original quote. Frank can run hundreds of interviews overnight and supports 50+ interview languages. It is SOC 2 Type II and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security).

MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse (tested), plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.

**Choose Frank over Conveo** when you don't need video, you already have customers to invite, and you want to start on a free plan. **Stay with Conveo** if you need video-first interviews or integrated recruiting through Respondent.io; Frank does not recruit participants.

### 2. Listen Labs: recruiting and moderation at enterprise scale

Listen Labs pairs an AI moderator with a participant network it describes as 50M+, and says its interviews run in 120+ languages ([listenlabs.com](https://listenlabs.com/)). It suits larger teams that want recruiting and interviewing from one vendor.

### 3. Outset: many formats and big panels

Outset supports text, voice, video and voice-to-voice responses, 40+ languages, and custom plans ([Outset, Pricing](https://outset.ai/pricing)). Its recruiting page lists 25+ panel integrations and also lets you send your own interview link to your users ([Outset, Recruit](https://outset.ai/platform/recruit)).

### 4. Koji: self-serve, with voice, chat or phone

Koji runs AI interviews by voice, chat or phone in 30+ languages, with self-recruiting or a panel, and documents an MCP connector ([koji.so](https://www.koji.so/)). Its homepage lists GDPR, a HIPAA BAA, and SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022 controls "verified by Aikido" (same source). Koji's own Conveo comparison gives its voice pricing as €79/month for 79 credits, at 3 credits per voice interview ([Koji blog](https://www.koji.so/blog/conveo-alternatives-2026)).

### 5. Usercall: published self-serve pricing

Usercall's first interview is free, and Lite costs $89/month or $899/year with interviews up to 12 minutes; Core is $199/month and Pro $399/month with interviews up to 45 minutes ([Usercall, Pricing](https://www.usercall.co/pricing)). It supports 50+ languages (same source), and can recruit for you or let you bring your own participants ([Usercall, Recruit](https://www.usercall.co/recruit-participants)).

### 6. Strella: AI and human moderation with on-screen tasks

Strella combines AI moderation and live human moderation, and lets participants react to websites, images, videos and ad copy with screen and video recording ([Strella, Product](https://www.strella.io/product)). It fits studies where people must interact with something on screen.

## How to choose

1. **Do you need video?** If yes, compare Conveo, Outset and Strella. If voice is enough, Frank, Koji and Usercall are lighter to start.
2. **Do you need recruiting?** If you have no audience, choose a tool with a panel. If you have customers, a link-based tool is simpler; Frank does not recruit participants.
3. **Do you need a published price?** Frank, Usercall and Koji publish plan prices. Conveo publishes typical Respondent.io recruiting costs but not a platform price list; Listen Labs, Outset and Strella did not publish prices on the pages reviewed.
4. **Will you run research from an AI assistant?** Check MCP support and which clients have been tested.
