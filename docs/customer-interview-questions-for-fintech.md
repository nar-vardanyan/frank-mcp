## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand hesitation, failed or delayed money movement, or why a customer would change providers. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name customers use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite customers from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you moved money or ran a payment with [product]. What were you trying to get done?
2. Where did you hesitate, and what made you continue or stop?
3. Walk me through a time a transfer, payout, or reconciliation did not go as expected.
4. What do you check before you trust a financial product with a real transaction?
5. Who else has to sign off, and what do they worry about?
6. Tell me about a moment when a fee, delay, or error caused a problem for you or a customer.
7. What would make you move this workflow to a different provider?
8. If you could change one thing about how money moves through this product, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a fintech customer interview?

Ask about a recent payment, payout, or transfer: where they hesitated, what they check before they trust a product with real money, what a delay or error meant, and what would make them change providers. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit fintech customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite customers from your own product or email list.

### Why interview about trust and money movement?

A support ticket or a declined payment can show that something failed. An interview can show where the customer hesitated, who else had to sign off, and what would make them move the workflow. Use the guide when that story would change a product or operations decision.
