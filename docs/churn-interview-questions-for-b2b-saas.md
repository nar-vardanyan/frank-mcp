Use these question groups soon after a cancel, downgrade, or failed renewal, while the decision is still easy to recall. Invite contacts from those accounts, not only the person who clicked cancel. ## Questions: the trigger

Use this group to get the event that started the cancel or downgrade, not a generic list of likes and dislikes.

1. Walk me through the moment you first thought about cancelling or downgrading [product]. What had just happened?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: Was that a single incident, a slow fade in use, or a change in your team or budget?
2. What almost made you stay at that point?
   Follow up: Who argued for staying, and what did they need to see?
3. When you compared the cost of keeping [product] with the cost of leaving, what did you count?
   Follow up: What did you leave out of that comparison that you noticed later?

## Questions: who decided, and what replaced it

Use this group when a buying committee, a new leader, or a replacement tool may have driven the outcome.

1. Who else was involved in the decision to leave, and what did each person care about?
   Follow up: Who could have reversed it, and what would they have needed?
2. What did you switch to, including going back to a spreadsheet or doing nothing? What does that give you that [product] did not?
   Follow up: What is worse now than it was before?
3. What was painful about moving data, workflows, or people off [product]?
   Follow up: What would have made that move unnecessary?

## Questions: what would have kept them

Use this group when you can still change packaging, onboarding, or success motions for customers who look like this account.

1. Looking back, what should we have noticed earlier, and how should we have shown up?
   Follow up: Was there a moment you would have taken a save conversation if we had asked?
2. What would have had to change in the product, the plan, or the working relationship for you to renew?
   Follow up: Which of those changes was required, and which would only have helped?
3. If a peer in a similar role were about to buy [product] today, what would you tell them to watch for?
   Follow up: What would you tell them we actually do well?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a B2B SaaS churn interview?

Ask what triggered the cancel or downgrade, who else was involved, what they switched to, what the move cost, and what would have kept them. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide, and talk about the recent decision rather than a general review of the product.

### Does Frank recruit cancelled B2B customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite contacts from accounts that already cancelled or downgraded, for example from your CRM or cancel list.

### When should I send a churn interview?

Send it soon after the cancel, downgrade, or failed renewal, while the decision is still easy to recall. Invite more than the person who clicked cancel when other people took part in the decision.
