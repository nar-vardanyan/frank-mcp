## When to use this guide

Use these question groups after a student or teacher drops mid-term, after a class stops assigning the product, or after a school or district does not renew. Invite people from rosters, admin contacts, or email lists you already have. Frank does not recruit participants. Be careful with minors: only invite participants you are already allowed to contact, and follow your own school's or product's consent rules. For interviews with active teachers, students, or admins, see [customer interview questions for edtech](customer-interview-questions-for-edtech.html).

## Questions: mid-term drop-off

Use this group with teachers or students who started the term and then stopped, or who completed much less than they planned.

1. Walk me through the last time [product] was part of class or homework, and the moment it stopped being part of the routine.
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: What else was competing for that time, and what broke first?
2. What made [product] hard to keep using during a normal week, such as roster issues, login, device access, or the length of the task?
   Follow up: What did you do instead that week?
3. When did you first think "we are not going to finish this"? What had just happened?
   Follow up: What would have helped you continue from that point?

## Questions: school or admin non-renewal

Use this group with the people who decided not to renew, or who advised that decision, including a curriculum lead or IT admin.

1. Walk me through the renewal conversation. What was on the table, and what decided it?
   Follow up: Who else was involved, and what did each person need to see?
2. What outcomes had you hoped to show by the end of the term, and where did the evidence fall short?
   Follow up: Was the gap in usage, in learning evidence, in training, or in how [product] fit the existing tools?
3. What would have needed to change in the product, the rollout, or the contract for you to renew?
   Follow up: What did [product] still do well enough that you were sorry to lose it?

## Questions: setup, roster, and classroom fit

Use this group when LMS, single sign-on, rostering, or classroom workflow is part of why people left.

1. Tell me about getting [product] set up for a class: rosters, logins, and the first assignment.
   Follow up: Where did that cost you time you did not have?
2. How did [product] fit beside the tools you already required, such as your LMS or gradebook?
   Follow up: Where did work get entered twice, or not at all?
3. If a peer were adopting [product] next term, what would you tell them to put in place in the first two weeks?
   Follow up: What should they not spend time on?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in an edtech churn interview?

Ask about the moment use dropped during the term, what made a normal week hard, how the renewal was decided, and whether setup, rostering, or classroom fit played a part. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit students, teachers, or school admins?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite people from rosters, admin contacts, or email lists you already have. Only invite participants you are already allowed to contact.

### Can I interview students who are minors?

Only if you are already allowed to contact them and you follow your school's or product's consent rules. Frank does not recruit students. If you cannot invite the student, invite a teacher or admin from the same account and ask about the classroom pattern, not about a child you should not contact.
