# Listen Labs Alternatives: 8 AI-Moderated Interview Platforms Compared (2026)

**Last updated:** Nov 27, 2026. Competitor facts accessed Nov 27, 2026.

**Short answer:** The main Listen Labs alternatives are Frank AI Researcher, Usercall, Outset, Strella, User Intuition, GetWhy, Conveo and Voicepanel. For recruited video studies, Outset, Strella and GetWhy are the closest match. For AI-moderated voice interviews with customers you already have, with a Free plan and published prices, Frank AI Researcher fits, and it is [available as an app in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168).

> **Disclosure:** we make Frank AI Researcher. See [How we compared](#how-we-compared).

## Listen Labs at a glance

Listen Labs runs AI-moderated video interviews with dynamic follow-up questions ([source](https://userguide.listenlabs.ai/), accessed Nov 27, 2026). Languages: 120+ languages (homepage); the user guide says 100+ ([source](https://listenlabs.com/), accessed Nov 27, 2026). Participants: Global network of 50M+ participants, including hard-to-reach audiences, or your own list of contacts ([source](https://listenlabs.com/), accessed Nov 27, 2026). Pricing: No public price list (listenlabs.com/pricing returned 404); the user guide describes a platform fee plus interview credits per completed interview ([source](https://userguide.listenlabs.ai/), accessed Nov 27, 2026). ChatGPT and MCP: Listen Labs MCP for ChatGPT (OpenAI App Store), Claude (connector directory) and Codex, with OAuth ([source](https://docs.listenlabs.com/mcp-docs/connect), accessed Nov 27, 2026).

**When Listen Labs is the right pick:** you need large studies with recruiting built in, including niche and B2B audiences, video interviews, and very wide language coverage. Teams usually compare alternatives when they want published prices, a free way to start, or voice interviews with customers they already have.

## Quick picks

- **Voice interviews with your own customers, a Free plan and research from ChatGPT or Claude:** Frank AI Researcher (our product).
- **Self-serve voice interviews from published monthly plans, with optional recruiting:** Usercall.
- **Several response formats and research-team support:** Outset.
- **AI plus human moderation and video highlight reels:** Strella.
- **Pay per interview, with an optional vetted panel:** User Intuition.
- **Video interviews with global recruitment:** GetWhy.
- **Enterprise voice and video consumer research:** Conveo.
- **A fully managed project:** Voicepanel.

## FAQ

### What are the main Listen Labs alternatives?

Frank AI Researcher, Usercall, Outset, Strella, User Intuition, GetWhy, Conveo and Voicepanel. Outset, Strella and GetWhy are closest for recruited video studies; Frank AI Researcher and Usercall suit self-serve voice interviews; User Intuition prices per interview; Voicepanel runs managed projects.

### Does Listen Labs publish pricing?

Not as a price list. On Nov 27, 2026, listenlabs.com/pricing returned 404. Listen's user guide describes a platform fee plus interview credits per completed interview, with more credits for harder-to-reach audiences.

### Which Listen Labs alternatives publish prices?

Frank AI Researcher (Starter $49/mo billed annually or $59 monthly, plus a Free plan), Usercall (Lite $89/mo or $899 yearly), User Intuition ($30 per quality voice interview with your own sample, $60 with its panel) and Voicepanel (projects from $5,000). Outset lists custom pricing; Strella, GetWhy and Conveo do not list prices on the pages we opened.

### Is there a Listen Labs alternative for interviewing my own customers?

Yes. Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews only with customers you invite, from a link or website widget. The Free plan includes 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month. Listen Labs also lets you use your own list of contacts, so this matters most if you do not need its participant network.

### Which Listen Labs alternatives work in ChatGPT?

Frank AI Researcher is available as an app in ChatGPT. Strella says it is a connector in ChatGPT, and Outset, Usercall and User Intuition say their MCP servers connect to ChatGPT. Listen Labs itself is in the OpenAI App Store per its docs. Works with ChatGPT, Claude and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.
