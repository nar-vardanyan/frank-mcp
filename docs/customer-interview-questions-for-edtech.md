## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when a workflow spans teachers, students, and admins, and you need to see who adopts the product and where the work breaks. Interview one group at a time with the same core questions, and note which role each person has. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite people from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [product] in a real class, school day, or admin task. What were you trying to get done?
2. Who else is involved in choosing or using it (teacher, student, parent, or admin), and what does each person care about?
3. Walk me through how a new person first starts using it. Where do they get stuck?
4. What did you use before this, and what do you still do outside it, such as paper, another tool, or a workaround?
5. Tell me about a time it got in the way of teaching, learning, or school operations.
6. What almost made you stop using it, or use it only for some classes or students?
7. What would need to be true for the other group to use it the way you hoped?
8. If you could change one step in the workflow, which step and why?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in an edtech customer interview?

Ask about a recent class, school day, or admin task: who chooses and uses the product, where a new person gets stuck, when it got in the way, and which step they would change. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit teachers, students, or admins?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite teachers, students, or admins from your own product or email list.

### Should I interview teachers and admins with the same guide?

Use the same core questions so you can compare answers, and interview one group at a time. Note which role each person has, and follow up on the steps that group actually performs.
