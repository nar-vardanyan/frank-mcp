# Best AI User-Interview Tools in 2026

**In short:** The best AI user-interview tool in 2026 depends on whether you need a recruited panel, video or on-screen testing, or a way to interview customers you already have. Frank is a strong fit for AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers, with a published free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free; Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, and several others are stronger when you need recruiting, video, or prototype testing.

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai), a voice-first product built by Prelaunch Group, Inc. The product website is https://www.hifrank.ai (prelaunch.com is the parent company, not Frank's site). It runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. This page compares Frank with other well-known AI-moderated interview tools, using each company's live public pages, opened on 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan). It is not a paid ranking, and it is not a complete catalog of every tool in the category. Prices and feature lists change, so check each site before you buy.

## When to choose which

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a shareable [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), including overnight. Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, and works today in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse. It states 50+ interview languages, and states SOC 2 Type II and [GDPR](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security) compliance. Frank does not recruit participants, and on-screen prototype testing is not stated on the Frank pages reviewed.

**Choose Outset** when you need a large recruited sample, video or voice-to-voice interviews, on-screen usability or prototype testing, digital twins, or researcher-led live interviews in the same platform. Outset's recruit page says you can reach over 1.1 billion participants in over 85 countries through 25+ panel integrations. Outset also states HIPAA and ISO 42001 compliance alongside SOC 2 Type II and GDPR. Pricing is custom; there is no public starting price on the pages reviewed.

**Choose Listen Labs** when you want an end-to-end enterprise research workflow: recruiting from a network Listen describes as 50M+ participants in 70+ countries, video interviews with concept and prototype testing, Quality Guard (Listen says it will replace a low-quality complete at no extra cost), and deliverables such as slide decks and highlight reels. Listen's own pages describe 120+ interview and reporting languages on the homepage and a product announcement (other Listen pages say 100+ or 110+). Listen states SOC 2 Type II plus ISO 42001, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701, with optional EU hosting. There is no public dollar price list.

**Choose Strella** when you need on-screen tasks — websites, prototypes, card sorting, or tree testing — or when you want a human moderator and an AI moderator in the same product. Strella bills on usage rather than a published monthly minute plan. Its own pages state two different panel figures (over 8 million on the product page; 100M+ across 24 panel partners on the AI-moderation page). Public pages say Strella is SOC 2 compliant and GDPR ready, without "Type II" on the pages reviewed.

**Choose Usercall** when you want self-serve recruiting and on-screen mockup or Figma testing, with a published price list. Usercall's first interview is free, paid plans start at $89 per month, and Usercall says recruiting reaches 95M+ people across panel partners in 151 countries. Interview caps go up to 45 minutes on Pro, or up to one hour on request. A hosted MCP connector is documented. A SOC 2 claim was not stated on the Usercall pages reviewed.

**Choose Conveo** when you want a video-first interview platform with integrated recruitment (including Respondent.io), ISO 27001 and EU hosting alongside SOC 2 Type II and GDPR, and an MCP server for assistants. Conveo does not publish a full interview-platform price list on the pages reviewed.

**Choose User Intuition** when you want published per-interview prices and will pay only for interviews that pass quality scoring. Starter voice interviews are $30 with your own sample or $60 with the standard panel, with no monthly fee, and the site offers three quality interviews to start. Professional is listed at $2,499 per month with 100 quality voice interviews included. Interviews can be chat, voice, or video, and a researcher can watch and steer a session live.

**Choose Koji** when you want AI interviews by voice, chat, or phone, in 30+ languages, with either a shareable link or a managed panel, and an MCP connector. Koji states SOC 2 Type II and mentions GDPR and CCPA. Koji publishes a pricing page described as plans from free to enterprise; specific prices were not readable in the static HTML fetched on 18 November 2026.

**Choose Voicepanel** when you want a white-glove study or an annual platform license with built-in panel recruiting. Voicepanel's pricing page says pay-per-project white-glove work starts at $5,000, and an enterprise license starts at $30,000. Pay-per-project is described as fully executed, not self-serve. Voicepanel also documents an MCP server and a SOC 2 Type II report.

## Pricing, in brief

Figures below are quoted as they appeared on 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan). Prices change.

A like-for-like monthly cost also depends on interview length, whether you pay for recruiting and incentives, and whether you need video or on-screen testing. None of these comparisons is complete until you add those costs for your study.

## How Frank fits

Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you can already reach. You set a research goal, share a link or website widget, let conversations run in parallel (including overnight), and read transcripts and themes tied back to the original quotes. Frank can run hundreds of interviews overnight with those customers. It does not find the people for you.

If you already work in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct or Muse, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on any plan, including Free. New research is created as a draft and is not published to participants until you approve it. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews), [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

If you have no customers yet, or if the study itself has to show a prototype on screen, one of the recruiting-and-testing tools above is the more specific fit.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI user-interview tool in 2026?
There is no single best tool for every team. The right choice depends on whether you need recruiting, video or on-screen testing, a published price list, or a way to interview customers you already have. Frank is a strong fit for AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, and Usercall are stronger when you need a panel or prototype testing.

### Which AI interview tool has a free plan?
As of 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan), Frank publishes a recurring Free plan with 60 voice minutes per month and no credit card. Usercall offers a free first interview. User Intuition offers three quality interviews to start. Several other vendors show "try for free" or "start for free" on their sites, but they do not all publish a recurring free plan on the pages reviewed.

### Which tools recruit interview participants?
Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, Conveo, User Intuition, Koji, and Voicepanel all document a way to recruit, usually through panel partners, as well as a way to bring your own participants. Frank does not recruit. You invite customers you already have.

### Which tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Frank, Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, Conveo, User Intuition, Koji, and Voicepanel all document an MCP connector on the pages reviewed. Frank states that MCP is included on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse (tested), plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. The other vendors do not all state plan-by-plan inclusion. Listen Labs says existing customers should contact their account team for MCP access.

### Is Frank a good AI interview tool for startups?
Frank can be a good fit when a startup already has users to invite and wants to start without a custom quote: the Free plan needs no credit card, paid plans start at $59 per month (or $49 per month billed yearly), and MCP is included on every plan. It is a weaker fit if the startup still needs a recruited panel or on-screen prototype testing.

### How should I compare security claims?
Compare what each vendor states on a live page, and ask for the report if you need it for procurement. As of 18 November 2026, Frank states SOC 2 Type II and GDPR. Outset, Listen Labs, Conveo, Koji, User Intuition, and Voicepanel also state SOC 2 Type II on the pages reviewed, and several of those vendors list additional certifications such as ISO or HIPAA. Strella states SOC 2 compliance without "Type II" on the pages reviewed. A SOC 2 claim was not stated on the Usercall pages reviewed. User Intuition states SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA compliance on its security page.
