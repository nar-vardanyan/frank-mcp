## When to choose which

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a shareable [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site). Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, and supports multilingual interviews. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. Frank does not recruit participants. On-screen prototype testing is not documented on the Frank pages reviewed.

**Choose Outset** when you need a large recruited sample, video or voice-to-voice interviews, on-screen usability or prototype testing, digital twins, or researcher-led live interviews in the same platform. Outset's recruit page says you can reach over 1.1 billion participants in over 85 countries through 25+ panel integrations. Outset also states HIPAA and ISO 42001 compliance alongside SOC 2 Type II and GDPR, on pages opened 20 November 2026. Pricing is custom; there is no public starting price on the pages reviewed 20 November 2026.

**Choose Listen Labs** when you want recruiting from a network the homepage describes as 50M+ possible respondents, video interviews, and deliverables such as slide decks and highlight reels. Quality Guard, on a page opened 20 November 2026, says Listen will replace a low-quality complete at no extra cost and that you can recruit in 70+ countries. Listen's homepage says 120+ languages (20 November 2026); other Listen pages said 100+ or 110+ on 20 November 2026. Listen states SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA, plus ISO 42001, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701, with optional EU hosting, on pages opened 20 November 2026. There is no public dollar price list.

**Choose Strella** when you need on-screen tasks (websites, prototypes, card sorting, or tree testing), or when you want a human moderator and an AI moderator in the same product. Strella does not publish a dollar price list. Its billing docs describe wallet credits, pay-as-you-go card checkout, or an enterprise invoice. Its own pages state different panel figures: over 8 million on the product page, 100M+ across 24 panel partners on the AI-moderation page, and 100M+ worldwide on the homepage. Public pages opened 20 November 2026 say Strella is SOC 2 compliant and GDPR ready, without "Type II" on the pages reviewed.

**Choose Usercall** when you want self-serve recruiting and on-screen mockup or Figma testing, with a published price list. Usercall's first interview is free, paid plans start at $89 per month, and Usercall says recruiting reaches 95M+ people across panel partners in 151 countries. The pricing page lists interviews up to 45 minutes on one plan, and says any plan can run up to one hour on request. A hosted MCP connector is documented. A SOC 2 claim was not documented on the Usercall pricing and recruit pages opened 20 November 2026.

**Choose Conveo** when you want a video-first interview platform with recruitment via CSV lists, links, QR codes, WhatsApp invites, or panels such as Respondent.io. The product page opened 20 November 2026 lists GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, and ISO 27001:2022, and says Conveo does not train on client data. EU hosting is not documented on that page. MCP docs exist at conveo.ai/docs/api-reference/mcp. A public interview-platform price list is not documented; on 20 November 2026 the pricing URL redirected to book-a-demo.

**Choose User Intuition** when you want published per-interview prices and will pay only for interviews that pass quality scoring. Starter voice interviews are $30 with your own sample or $60 with the standard panel, with no monthly fee, and the site offers three quality interviews to start. Professional is listed at $2,499 per month with 100 quality voice interviews included. Interviews can be chat, voice, or video, and a researcher can watch and steer a session live. Figures are from the pricing page opened 20 November 2026. Its security page opened 20 November 2026 states SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, and a HIPAA BAA on request.

**Choose Koji** when you want AI interviews by voice, chat, or phone, in 30+ languages, with either a shareable link or a managed panel, and an MCP connector. Koji states SOC 2 Type II and mentions GDPR and CCPA. Koji publishes a pricing page described as plans from free to enterprise; specific prices were not readable in the static HTML fetched on 20 November 2026.

**Choose Voicepanel** when you want a white-glove study or an annual platform license with built-in panel recruiting. Voicepanel's pricing page says pay-per-project white-glove work starts at $5,000, and an enterprise license starts at $30,000. Pay-per-project is described as fully executed, not self-serve. REST API and MCP access are listed under Enterprise. A SOC 2 Type II report is listed on the pricing page.

**Glaut** documents AI-moderated voice interviews and a choice between voice and typing, in 50+ languages. The product page says you can recruit participants. A panel size, public prices, and MCP were not documented. Pricing URLs returned 404. The call to action on the product page is "Request a demo."

**Userology** describes AI-moderated interviews, including generative interviews or a voice survey, prototype testing, and video calls. The homepage says AI recruits from a "10Mn+ participant pool." The homepage says interviews run in 40+ languages. Public dollar prices and MCP were not documented on the homepage and pricing page opened 20 November 2026. The pricing page call to action is "Book a free demo."

**Great Question** is a research suite. Self-serve includes interviews, surveys, and unmoderated prototype testing. AI-moderated interviews are an Enterprise add-on. You can recruit your own customers at no per-participant fee. The User Interviews panel is listed as an add-on. Self-serve is $129 per seat per month, or $1,290 per seat per year (up to 5 seats), with a 14-day free trial and no free plan. Enterprise is custom. Languages were not documented on the pricing page opened 20 November 2026. MCP is listed on the pricing page. Which plan includes it is not documented on Great Question's site as of Nov 2026.

On-screen prototype or usability testing is documented by Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, Userology, and Great Question. It is not documented on the Frank pages reviewed.

## How Frank fits

Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you can already reach. You set a research goal, share a link or website widget, let conversations run in parallel, and read transcripts and themes tied back to the original quotes. It does not find the people for you.

MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. You can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on any plan, including Free. New research is created as a draft and is not published to participants until you approve it. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews), [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

If you have no customers yet, or if the study itself has to show a prototype on screen, one of the recruiting-and-testing tools above is the more specific fit.

## FAQ

### What is Frank AI Researcher?

Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. You share an interviewer link or website widget, then read transcripts and themes. It supports multilingual interviews.

### Does Frank recruit participants?

No. Frank does not recruit participants. Several other tools in this comparison do, including Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, Conveo, User Intuition, Koji, Voicepanel, Userology and Glaut, according to those companies' own pages. Great Question's pricing page says you can recruit your own customers at no per-participant fee, and lists the User Interviews panel as an add-on.

Frank publishes a Free plan with 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month and MCP included. Great Question has a 14-day free trial and no free plan. Usercall offers a free first interview, and User Intuition offers three quality interviews to start, on pages opened 20 November 2026. Voicepanel's published starting prices are $5,000 per white-glove project or $30,000 per year for Enterprise. Koji's pricing page was live on 20 November 2026, but specific prices were not readable in the static HTML. Strella does not publish a public dollar price list.

### Is MCP included on Frank's Free plan?

Yes. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.

### How should a team choose an AI user-interview tool in 2026?

Match the tool to the job. The choice depends on whether you need recruiting, video or on-screen testing, a published price list, or a way to interview customers you already have. Frank is a fit for AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, and Usercall are a fit when you need a panel or prototype testing.

### Which tools work with AI assistants like Claude?

Frank includes MCP on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. Outset, Listen Labs, Strella, Usercall, Conveo, User Intuition, Koji, Voicepanel, and Great Question also document an MCP connection on the pages reviewed. Voicepanel and Great Question list MCP on Enterprise. Listen Labs says existing customers should contact their account team. Plan-by-plan inclusion is not documented for the others. Glaut and Userology MCP were not documented on the pages opened 20 November 2026.

### Is Frank a fit for a startup that already has users?

Frank can be a fit when a startup already has users to invite and wants to start without a custom quote: the Free plan needs no credit card, Starter is $49/month billed yearly, and MCP is included on every plan. It is a weaker fit if the startup still needs a recruited panel or on-screen prototype testing.

### How should I compare security claims?

Compare what each vendor states on a live page, and ask for the report if you need it for procurement. Outset, Listen Labs, Conveo, Koji, User Intuition, and Voicepanel also state SOC 2 Type II on the pages reviewed, and several of those vendors list additional certifications such as ISO or HIPAA. Strella states SOC 2 compliance without "Type II" on the pages reviewed 20 November 2026. A SOC 2 claim was not documented on the Usercall pages reviewed. Security claims for Glaut, Userology, and Great Question were not documented on the pages opened 20 November 2026.
