## Marketing questions interviews can answer

- **Why customers chose you,** and what else they considered, including doing nothing.
- **The words they use** for the problem, the outcome, and the alternatives, which you can test in copy and positioning.
- **Campaign or page mismatch:** what people expected from an ad or landing page, and what they found.
- **Voice of customer** for messaging decisions. See [voice of customer](glossary/voice-of-customer.html).

Campaign metrics tell you how many people clicked or converted. Interviews tell you why.

## Who to invite

- Recent buyers, while the decision is fresh.
- New sign-ups from a specific campaign.
- Customers who came through a page you are about to rewrite.

Invite them by email, in-app message, or a website widget. Frank does not recruit participants or provide an audience, so the invite comes from your own lists and channels.

## Turn transcripts into messaging inputs

**Draft a "why they chose us" study**

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to interview customers who bought [product] in the last 60 days. Write 6 to 8 open questions about the purchase: what was happening when they started looking, what else they considered, what made them choose us, what almost stopped them, and how they would describe us to a colleague. Do not ask them to rate our marketing. Wait for me to review the guide.
```

**Pull the customer's own words**

```text
From Frank, read the transcripts in my [study name] study. List the exact phrases customers used for the problem, the outcome they wanted, and the alternatives they considered, with the interview each came from. Then compare them with our current homepage copy: [paste]. Where do our words differ from theirs?
```

## Keep it honest

Report interview findings as what the people you spoke with said, not as a share of all customers. Check each quote in the full transcript before it goes into a brief, and ask a customer before you quote them by name in public.

## What it does not do

- **No audience recruiting or panel.** You invite your own customers.
- **No ad, creative, or landing page design tests.** Frank does not show images, designs, or prototypes.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **Not a survey tool.** Use a survey when you need to know how many people feel a certain way.
