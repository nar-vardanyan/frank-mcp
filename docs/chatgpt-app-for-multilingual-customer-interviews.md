## One core guide, several markets

- Keep the core questions identical across markets so answers can be compared.
- Avoid idioms, sports metaphors, and product jargon that will not survive translation.
- Have a native speaker adapt each version for meaning, tone, and formality, not only grammar.
- Take the interview in each language yourself, or with a native speaker, before anyone else does.

Because Frank supports multilingual interviews, one study can reach several markets without hiring a moderator for each.

## Using ChatGPT for the language work

ChatGPT can help you draft the core guide and spot wording that will not travel. A native speaker should still review each language.

```text
Here is my core interview guide: [paste]. Flag idioms, metaphors, product jargon, and questions that are long or stacked, which may not translate well into [languages]. Suggest plainer wording for each. Do not translate yet.
```

## Invite customers in their own language

Write the invite in the participant's language and say plainly which language the interview will be in. Use channels you already have: customer email, in-app messages, or a website widget on a localized page. Frank does not recruit participants, so you need customers you can already reach in each market.

## Analyze across markets

Read transcripts per market first, then compare. Group answers into themes across languages, but check each theme you act on against the original-language quote, ideally with a native speaker. Translated summaries can flatten nuance, such as politeness that hides a strong complaint.

```text
From Frank, read the transcripts in my [study name] study. Summarize themes for each market separately, keeping one original-language quote and an English translation for each theme. Then list where markets differ. Mark themes from a single interview as low confidence.
```

In a market where a few large accounts matter a lot, use a local human moderator for those conversations.

## What it does not do

- **No participant recruiting in any market.** You invite customers you already reach.
- **No replacement for a native speaker's review** of the guide and of key quotes.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
- **No automatic publishing.** A study drafted in ChatGPT stays a draft until you approve it.
