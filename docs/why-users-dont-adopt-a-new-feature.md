## How it works

1. Use your own product data or email list to find people who were shown a feature and did not use it. Frank does not recruit participants.
2. Write a short guide: whether they noticed the feature, what they expected, where they hesitated, and whether it solves a problem they actually have.
3. Share an interviewer link, or embed a website widget, so they can talk when they have time, without booking a call.
4. Read the transcripts and themes. Check any theme you will act on against exact quotes.

Product analytics can show who was exposed to a feature and who used it. Interviews are for the story behind a skipped or abandoned step.

## FAQ

### How do I find out why users don't adopt a new feature?

Interview people who saw the feature but did not use it. Invite them from your own product or email list, share an AI-moderated voice interview link so they can talk when they have time, and read the transcripts and themes.

### Does Frank recruit users for these interviews?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite users you already have, such as people who were shown a feature and did not adopt it.

### Should I look at product analytics too?

Analytics can show who was exposed to a feature and who used it. Interviews are for the story behind a skipped or abandoned step. The two methods work together; Frank only runs the interviews.
