## What does ChatGPT do well for UX research on its own?

- Turning a research question into a first draft of an interview guide.
- Rewording leading questions into open ones.
- Clustering notes or transcripts that you paste in, and proposing themes.
- Drafting a short readout from themes you have already checked.

## What can ChatGPT not do alone?

It cannot hold a conversation with your user while you are away. It also cannot tell you whether a theme appeared in one interview or in ten unless you give it the transcripts. That is the gap an interview product fills.

## How do you run UX research interviews from ChatGPT with Frank?

1. Add the [Frank app in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ad8ab4844a881919c79bb0864ffe168) and sign in.
2. Ask ChatGPT to draft a study. A study created over MCP is always a draft.
3. Review the guide and take the interview yourself.
4. Approve publishing, then send the voice interview link to users you already have, or embed the website widget in your product.
5. Ask ChatGPT for the themes, with quotes, and open the full transcripts to check them.

## Which prompt works for a UX research study?

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study about how [user type] complete [task] today. Ask about the last time they did it: what they were trying to do, where they got stuck, what they did next, and what they use instead. Do not describe our design. Keep the interview to about 5 minutes and keep it as a draft until I approve it.
```

## When should you not use it?

- **You need to watch someone use a prototype or a screen.** Frank runs voice interviews and does not show designs or run usability tests. Use a usability testing tool for that.
- **The topic is sensitive.** Moderate those yourself.
- **You need counts.** Interviews find reasons. A survey or product analytics tells you how many people share them.
