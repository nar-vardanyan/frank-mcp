## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide after a recent win, loss, renewal, or switch, when you need to know how the buying committee decided and what a change would cost. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name buyers use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite buyers from deals or accounts you already have. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Walk me through the last time your team evaluated a tool like [product]. What prompted the search?
2. Who else was involved in the decision, and what did each person care about?
3. What options did you compare, including staying with what you already had?
4. What almost blocked the purchase, or almost made you choose something else?
5. Tell me about the last time you switched away from a tool in this category. What triggered the switch?
6. What was painful about moving data, workflows, or people off the old tool?
7. After you bought, what took longer than you expected before the team got value?
8. If you were choosing again tomorrow, what would you need to see earlier?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a B2B SaaS customer interview?

Ask about a recent evaluation or switch: what prompted the search, who else was involved, what almost blocked the purchase, what the switch cost, and what they would need to see earlier next time. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit B2B buyers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite buyers from deals or accounts you already have, for example from your CRM or email list.

### Who should I invite from the buying committee?

Invite people from deals or accounts you already have who took part in the decision, not only the contract signer. Different people often care about price, security, implementation, or daily use. Frank does not recruit a panel of new buyers.
