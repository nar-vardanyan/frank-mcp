# Frank vs Strella: AI-Moderated Interviews Compared

**In short:** Frank and Strella both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts, themes, and quotes you can check against the original conversation. Choose Strella if you need a recruited panel, on-screen usability or prototype testing, human-led interviews in the same platform, or usage-based billing; choose Frank if you want to interview customers you already have, starting from a free plan with published prices, with MCP included on every plan, including Free.

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai), a voice-first product built by Prelaunch Group, Inc. The product website is https://www.hifrank.ai (prelaunch.com is the parent company, not Frank's site). It runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. Strella is a separate AI-powered customer research platform. This page compares them from both companies' live public pages, opened on 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan). Prices and feature lists change, so check each site before you buy.

## When to choose which

**Choose Strella** when you need people you do not already have, or when the interview itself has to show a website, prototype, image, video, or ad, or run classic UX methods such as card sorting and tree testing, with screen and video recording. Strella can also moderate the session with a human researcher instead of, or alongside, the AI moderator, and keeps both kinds of interviews in the same synthesis. Strella's public pages describe recruiting through panel partners (the product page says a global panel of over 8 million participants; the AI-moderation page says 100M+ participants across 24 panel partners — both figures are Strella's, and they are not the same), or you can bring your own customers. Billing is usage-based: wallet credits, pay-as-you-go card checkout, or an enterprise invoice, with incentives billed in addition to interview credits. Strella also documents an official MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other clients.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you do not need a panel or on-screen tasks. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), let voice conversations run (including overnight), and read the transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan with monthly voice minutes and no credit card, and MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Frank works today in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse, so you can set up a study and read transcripts from those assistants. Frank is SOC 2 Type II and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security). Frank also states 50+ interview languages, compared with the 46+ languages Strella lists. If you want many consistent voice conversations with your own customers, and you want a published monthly price list, that is the fit.

**Either tool can work** if you only need to share a link with people you already know, run interviews without a calendar, and read themes linked back to quotes. Both document an MCP connector, and both state SOC 2 and GDPR-related compliance (Frank states SOC 2 Type II; Strella's public pages say "SOC 2 compliant" or "SOC-II," without "Type II" on the pages reviewed). Those overlaps are why the choice usually comes down to recruiting, on-screen testing, human versus AI moderation, and how pricing is published, not to whether both can run an AI-moderated interview.

## How Frank works

You set a research goal and an interview guide. Frank suggests topics from that goal, then runs the voice interviews for you. You invite your own customers with a shareable interviewer link, or you embed a website widget so someone can start from your site. People join when they have time. There is no calendar to manage, and interviews can run in parallel, including overnight.

After each conversation you get a transcript. Answers are grouped into themes you can trace back to the original quotes. If you already work in an AI assistant, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) and ask it to help draft a study or read transcripts without leaving that chat. New research is created as a draft, so it is not published to participants until you approve it.

Frank can run hundreds of interviews overnight with customers you can already reach. It does not find those people for you. If you have no customers yet, you will need another way to recruit. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews) in a cancellation flow, [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

## How Strella works

On Strella's site, you set research objectives and Strella can draft an interview guide, or you can upload your own. You then either bring your own participants or place a panel order. AI-moderated interviews run around the clock and can run concurrently. The moderator adapts follow-ups in real time. If the study needs it, you can embed a stimulus or run a card sort or tree test, and capture the session on screen and video.

Strella also offers human moderation: your researchers lead a live session in the same platform, Strella handles recruiting and scheduling around it, and those sessions land in the same synthesis as the AI interviews.

After the conversations, Strella produces themes, transcripts, reports, and highlight reels. An official MCP connector lets assistants such as Claude or Cursor read projects and transcripts, and, with approval, create projects or draft recruitment orders ([MCP server](https://app.strella.io/docs/product/mcp), as of Nov 18, 2026). Plan-by-plan MCP inclusion is not stated on the pages reviewed.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between Frank and Strella?
Both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts and themes. The main difference is the job each one is set up to do. Strella can recruit participants, run on-screen usability and prototype tests, offer human-led interviews in the same platform, and bills on usage. Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you already have, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Frank does not recruit.

### When should I choose Strella?
Choose Strella when you need a recruited panel, when people must react to a website, prototype, image, or video during the interview, when you want card sorting or tree testing in the same study, when a researcher should moderate live, or when usage-based billing fits better than a monthly minute plan. Those capabilities are documented on Strella's own site and are the cases where Strella is the more specific fit.

### When should I choose Frank?
Choose Frank when you can already reach the customers you want to hear from and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a link or website widget, including overnight. Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, states 50+ interview languages, and states SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance. It is a weaker fit if you need recruiting, on-screen prototype or usability testing, or human-led interviews in the same tool.

### Does Frank recruit interview participants?
No. Frank does not recruit participants. Invite your own customers: people who recently signed up, cancelled, bought, or used the feature. If you have no customers yet, a recruiting service or your network is a better first step. Strella can recruit through panel partners or work with people you bring.

### Do both tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Yes. Frank is available as an [MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse (tested), plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. New Frank research is created as a draft and is not published to participants without your approval. Strella documents an official MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Figma Make, Cursor, Claude Code, and other MCP clients, with OAuth 2.0. Strella does not state on the pages reviewed whether MCP is included on every plan.

### How does Frank pricing compare with Strella?
As of 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan), Frank's Free plan includes 60 voice minutes per month with no credit card, and paid plans start at $59 per month, or $49 per month billed yearly. Strella does not publish a public dollar price list. It describes usage-based pricing, paid with wallet credits, a one-time card checkout, or an enterprise invoice, with incentives billed on top of interview credits. Prices change; check [Frank's pricing page](https://www.hifrank.ai/pricing) and ask Strella for a current quote before you buy.

### How do Frank and Strella handle security?
Frank is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant. The live security page also states AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, with access controls you manage. Strella's public pages say it is SOC 2 compliant and GDPR ready, supports SSO, and never trains on your data. "SOC 2 Type II" was not stated on the Strella pages reviewed, and www.strella.io/security and www.strella.io/trust were not live on 18 November 2026.
