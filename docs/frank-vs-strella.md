## When to choose which

**Choose Strella** when you need people you do not already have, or when the interview itself has to show a website, prototype, image, video, or ad, or run classic UX methods such as card sorting and tree testing, with screen and video recording. Strella can also moderate the session with a human researcher instead of, or alongside, the AI moderator, and keeps both kinds of interviews in the same synthesis. Strella's public pages describe recruiting through panel partners (the product page says a global panel of over 8 million participants; the AI-moderation page says 100M+ participants across 24 panel partners — both figures are Strella's, and they are not the same), or you can bring your own customers. Billing docs describe pre-purchased wallet credits, pay-as-you-go card checkout, or an enterprise invoice, with incentives billed in addition to interview credits. Strella also documents an official MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and other clients.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you do not need a panel or on-screen tasks. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), let voice conversations run, and read the transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan with monthly voice minutes and no credit card, and MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. Frank is SOC 2 and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security). Frank also states 50+ interview languages, compared with the 46+ languages Strella lists. If you want many consistent voice conversations with your own customers, and you want a published monthly price list, that is the fit.

**Either tool can work** if you need to share a link with people you already know, run interviews without a calendar, and read themes linked back to quotes. Both document an MCP connector, and both state SOC 2 and GDPR-related compliance (Frank states SOC 2; Strella's public pages say "SOC 2 compliant" or "SOC-II," without "Type II" on the pages reviewed). Those overlaps are why the choice usually comes down to recruiting, on-screen testing, human versus AI moderation, and how pricing is published, not to whether both can run an AI-moderated interview.

## How Frank works

You set a research goal and an interview guide. Frank suggests topics from that goal, then runs the voice interviews for you. You invite your own customers with a shareable interviewer link, or you embed a website widget so someone can start from your site. People join when they have time. There is no calendar to manage, and interviews can run in parallel.

After each conversation you get a transcript. Answers are grouped into themes you can trace back to the original quotes. If you already work in an AI assistant, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) and ask it to help draft a study or read transcripts without leaving that chat. New research is created as a draft, so it is not published to participants until you approve it.

Frank does not find participants for you. If you have no customers yet, you will need another way to recruit. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews) in a cancellation flow, [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

## How Strella works

On Strella's site, you set research objectives and Strella can draft an interview guide, or you can upload your own. You then either bring your own participants or place a panel order. AI-moderated interviews run around the clock and can run concurrently. The moderator adapts follow-ups in real time. If the study needs it, you can embed a stimulus or run a card sort or tree test, and capture the session on screen and video.

Strella also offers human moderation: your researchers lead a live session in the same platform, Strella handles recruiting and scheduling around it, and those sessions land in the same synthesis as the AI interviews.

After the conversations, Strella produces themes, transcripts, reports, and highlight reels. An official MCP connector lets assistants such as Claude or Cursor read projects and transcripts, and, with approval, create projects or draft recruitment orders ([MCP server](https://app.strella.io/docs/product/mcp), 20 Nov 2026). Plan-by-plan MCP inclusion is not documented on the pages reviewed.

## Pricing

Prices below are quoted as they appeared on each company's live pages on 20 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan). Prices change. Confirm the live page before you buy.

**Frank**, from [hifrank.ai/pricing](https://www.hifrank.ai/pricing), opened 20 Nov 2026:

- **Free:** 225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month. No credit card required.
- **Starter:** $49/month billed yearly.

MCP is included on all Frank plans, including Free.

**Strella**, from [strella.io/ai-moderation](https://www.strella.io/ai-moderation) and [Strella billing docs](https://app.strella.io/docs/product/account/billing) (www.strella.io/pricing redirected to the homepage on 20 November 2026):

- Strella does not publish a public dollar price list on the pages reviewed.
- Marketing pages describe "usage based pricing tiers."
- Docs list three ways to pay: pre-purchased wallet credits (available to every organization; credits do not expire until the organization is closed), pay-as-you-go card checkout (Strella says it does not store a card on file), or an invoice for enterprise customers (invoiced billing is not self-serve; you contact the account team).
- New organizations start at a $0 wallet balance until they purchase credits. Strella deducts credits when you place a panel order or pay out self-recruited participants.
- Participant incentives are billed in addition to interview credits. A panel order's total includes both.
- Dollar amounts for interview credits are not documented on the pages reviewed.

A like-for-like monthly cost is hard to state because Strella does not publish plan prices. Frank's published Starter is $49/month billed yearly, and Frank offers a recurring Free tier. Strella's cost will depend on interview credits, incentives, and whether you use a panel. Neither comparison is complete until you add those costs for your study.

## FAQ

### Is Frank a Strella alternative if I want to interview my own customers?

Yes, if you already have customers to invite and you want voice interviews. Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews from a shareable link or website widget and returns transcripts and themes. It has a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month) and MCP on every plan, including Free.

### Can Strella recruit participants?

Yes. Strella's product page says you can recruit from a global panel of over 8 million participants. The AI-moderation page and homepage also say 100M+ participants across 24 panel partners. Frank does not recruit participants.

### Does Strella publish prices?

Not as a public dollar price list. As of 20 November 2026, strella.io/pricing redirected to the homepage. Strella's billing docs describe pre-purchased wallet credits, pay-as-you-go card checkout, or an enterprise invoice. Frank publishes a Free plan and paid plans starting at $49/month billed yearly.

### Is MCP included on Frank's Free plan?

Yes. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.

### What is the difference between Frank and Strella?
Both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts and themes. The main difference is the job each one is set up to do. Strella can recruit participants, run on-screen usability and prototype tests, offer human-led interviews in the same platform, and bills through wallet credits, card checkout, or an enterprise invoice. Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you already have, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Frank does not recruit.

### When should I choose Strella?
Choose Strella when you need a recruited panel, when people must react to a website, prototype, image, or video during the interview, when you want card sorting or tree testing in the same study, when a researcher should moderate live, or when wallet or pay-as-you-go billing fits better than a monthly minute plan. Those capabilities are documented on Strella's own site and are the cases where Strella is the more specific fit.

### When should I choose Frank?
Choose Frank when you can already reach the customers you want to hear from and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a link or website widget. Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, states 50+ interview languages, and states SOC 2 and GDPR compliance. It is a weaker fit if you need recruiting, on-screen prototype or usability testing, or human-led interviews in the same tool.

### Do both tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Yes. Frank is available as an [MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. New Frank research is created as a draft and is not published to participants without your approval. Strella documents an official MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Figma Make, Cursor, Claude Code, and other MCP clients, with OAuth 2.0. Plan-by-plan inclusion for Strella MCP is not documented.

### How do Frank and Strella handle security?
Frank is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant. The live security page also states AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, with access controls you manage. Strella's public pages say it is SOC 2 compliant and GDPR ready, supports SSO, and never trains on your data. "SOC 2 Type II" was not documented on the Strella pages reviewed, and www.strella.io/security and www.strella.io/trust were not live on 20 November 2026.
