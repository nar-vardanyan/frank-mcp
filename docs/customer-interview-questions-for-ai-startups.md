## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand how a team evaluates an AI product, when they trust the output, or why they would switch. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name customers use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite customers from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Walk me through the last time your team evaluated a tool like [product]. What problem were you trying to solve?
2. How did you decide whether you could trust the model output for a real workflow?
3. What did you use to evaluate quality, and what almost made you say no?
4. Who else had to sign off, and what did they worry about?
5. Tell me about a time the product was wrong or unexpected. What did you do next?
6. What would it take to move this workflow to a different provider, including data, prompts, or evals you already have?
7. After you started using it, what took longer than you expected before the team trusted it in production?
8. If you were choosing again tomorrow, what would you need to see earlier?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in an AI startup customer interview?

Ask about a recent evaluation or switch: how they judged whether they could trust the model output, what they used to evaluate quality, what a switch would cost, and what they would need to see earlier next time. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit AI startup customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite customers from your own product or email list.

### Why interview about model trust and evaluation?

A usage log can show that someone tried the product. An interview can show how they judged the output, what almost made them say no, and what a switch would cost. Use the guide when that story would change a product or sales decision.
