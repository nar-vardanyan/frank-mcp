## When to use this guide

Use these question groups after an account closure, a stopped payout, a cancelled card or wallet, or a customer who moved a recurring flow elsewhere. Money and trust make people cautious, so keep questions about a specific event, and do not press for account numbers or credentials. Invite people from your own close or churn list. Frank does not recruit participants. For broader fintech interviews with active customers, see [customer interview questions for fintech](customer-interview-questions-for-fintech.html).

## Questions: where trust broke

Use this group when a failed payment, a freeze, a surprise fee, or a support delay may have started the exit.

1. Walk me through the moment you first thought about closing or leaving [product]. What had just happened with your money or your account?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: Was it a failed transfer, a hold, a fee, a notification you did not expect, or something else?
2. At that moment, what did you believe had gone wrong, and how sure were you that it would be fixed?
   Follow up: What would have restored your confidence that week?
3. Tell me about how you reached out for help, if you did. What was useful, and what made things worse?
   Follow up: How long did you wait, and what did you do while you waited?

## Questions: the close, and where the money went

Use this group when you need the replacement and the cost of leaving, not only the complaint.

1. Walk me through closing or stepping away from [product]. Which steps were clear, and which made you anxious?
   Follow up: What were you afraid might happen to a pending payment or to records you still needed?
2. Where did you move the activity [product] used to handle, and why there?
   Follow up: What does that option give you that [product] did not, including speed, fees, or a feeling of control?
3. What is harder now than it was before you left?
   Follow up: What would have had to be true for you to keep both, or to stay?

## Questions: fees, verification, and rules

Use this group when pricing, identity checks, or policy limits show up in cancellations.

1. Which fee, limit, or verification step played a part in your decision, if any?
   Follow up: When did you first notice it, and was it a surprise?
2. What felt unfair or hard to predict, even if you understood the rule later?
   Follow up: How should that have been explained before you hit it?
3. If you were advising a friend who was about to start with [product], what would you tell them to check first?
   Follow up: What would you tell them we handled well?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a fintech churn interview?

Ask about the moment trust broke, how help was offered, what the close felt like, where they moved the money flow, and whether a fee, limit, or verification step played a part. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide, and stay with a specific event.

### Does Frank recruit customers who closed a fintech account?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite people from your own close or churn list.

### How do I keep the interview from asking for sensitive account details?

Ask about the event and the decision, not account numbers, passwords, balances, or documents. If someone starts to share a credential or a full card number, stop that line and continue with how the event felt and what they did next.
