**In short:** To interview churned users quickly, invite people who recently cancelled from your own product or email list, then share an AI-moderated voice interview link so they can talk when they have time, without booking a call. ## How it works

1. Pick a recent group of people who cancelled, from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.
2. Write a short guide about what happened: when they signed up, when they started to doubt the product, what they tried, and what they use now.
3. Share an interviewer link, or embed a website widget, so they can talk when they have time, without booking a call.
4. Read the transcripts and themes. Check any theme you will act on against exact quotes.

A cancellation survey can count the first reason people click. Interviews are for the story behind a short reason code. For a longer method walkthrough, see [Churn interviews](churn-interviews.html) and [How to find out why customers churn](how-to-find-out-why-customers-churn.html).
