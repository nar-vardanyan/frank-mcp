## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand how law firm partners or in-house counsel decide they can use a product on confidential work, what almost blocked them, or why they would switch. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite law firm or in-house customers you already have. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Walk me through the last time you or your team used [product] on a real matter. What were you trying to get done?
2. Are you at a law firm or in-house, and who else has to sign off before a tool like this can be used?
3. What did you need to see about confidentiality, data handling, or privilege before you would try it on real work?
4. What almost blocked the purchase, or almost kept the tool from being used?
5. Tell me about a time the output was wrong, incomplete, or not usable as-is. What did you do next?
6. What would make you move this work to a different product, including concerns about confidentiality or switching cost?
7. After you bought, what took longer than you expected before attorneys actually used it?
8. If you were choosing again tomorrow, what would you need to see earlier?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a legaltech customer interview?

Ask about a recent matter: whether the person is at a law firm or in-house, what they needed to see about confidentiality before real work, who else had to sign off, and what would make them move the work. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit legaltech customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite law firm or in-house customers you already have.

### Why interview about confidentiality and who signs off?

A usage report can show that a tool was opened. An interview can show what counsel needed to see about confidentiality before real work, who else had to sign off, and what almost blocked use. Use the guide when that story would change a product or security decision.
