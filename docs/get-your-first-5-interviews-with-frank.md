# Get your first 5 interviews with Frank

**In short:** Your study is live, but interviews do not start on their own. Copy the interviewer link, test it once yourself, then send it to people you can already reach, with a short note that says how long it takes and why you want their voice. Frank does not recruit participants for you.

**We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai/?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=first5-kit)**, a voice-first product that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. This page is a share kit: where to send the link, three messages you can paste, and a short checklist.

This is the step most published studies stall on. In the 90 days to 26 November 2026, 19 people outside our team published a study; 9 of those studies had no participant call at all, and only a small number showed interviews from more than one person. The share step in the product currently ends at "copy link", so this kit is the missing next step.

The invite messages below say "10 minutes". If your interview is a different length, change the number before you send.

## Where to share your interview link

Use channels where you already have a relationship. You are asking people you know, not recruiting a panel.

- **Your customer email list.** This is the most direct place: people who already use or bought your product, especially those who recently signed up, became active, or cancelled. They can talk about a specific recent event, and email is how most teams already reach them.
- **A message to existing users in your product.** An in-app banner, tooltip, or inbox note reaches people while they are using the product, so they can start the interview without booking a call. You add this in your own product, not in Frank.
- **A LinkedIn post, plus a few DMs.** A public post can reach customers and peers who already follow you. A short DM is better for people you already know. There is a one-click LinkedIn compose link below, using the same format as Frank's share-kit emails: `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/?shareActive=true&text=` plus your encoded post.
- **Slack or Discord communities you are already in.** Share it only in a workspace or server where you are a known member and where asking for 10 minutes is normal. Do not drop the link into groups you do not belong to.
- **Your website, with Frank's floating widget.** The interview can start on your site, without the person leaving the page. In the Frank dashboard, open your study, click **Add to your site/app**, switch on **Activate widget**, set your domain, then **Copy code**. Only the Floating format is fully available today. The install code is unique to your study; you copy it from the dashboard, it is not something you can build from the study id. Setup help: [Embedding Frank on your site](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=first5-kit).

Your interviewer link looks like `https://app.hifrank.ai/research/{your-study-id}/interview?botType=audio`. The app's own copy-link button writes it in that form. The `botType=audio` parameter is optional and defaults to audio, but keeping it matches what the app copies.

## Three messages you can paste

Replace `{interview_link}` with your interviewer link. Replace the bracketed prompts, such as `[product]`, with your own words. If your study is not 10 minutes long, change the time before you send.

### Email

Subject: could you spare 10 minutes this week?

```
Hi [name],

Could you spare about 10 minutes this week? I'm doing short voice conversations with customers to make [product] better, and your experience would really help.

No meeting to book. Just open this link whenever it suits you:

{interview_link}

Thank you,
[your name]
```

**Optional extra:** If you are writing to someone who cancelled, the [invite email step](tools/cancellation-reason-sorter/#invite) in the cancellation reason sorter drafts a short email you can edit and copy, asking them for a call about why they left.

### LinkedIn

Post or DM:

```
This week I'm talking to people who [do the thing]. It's a 10-minute voice conversation you can take whenever you like, no call to schedule. Here's the link: {interview_link}
```

One-click compose, after you URL-encode the post (including your link):

```
https://www.linkedin.com/feed/?shareActive=true&text=I'm%20running%20a%20few%20short%20voice%20conversations%20this%20week.%20If%20you%20have%20a%20few%20minutes%2C%20I'd%20love%20to%20hear%20from%20you%3A%20{interview_link}
```

Logged-out visitors are sent to LinkedIn login and then back to this compose screen, with the text kept. That was checked with a logged-out request on 26 November 2026; a logged-in prefill was not tested.

### Slack (also works in Discord)

```
Hey, I'm trying to get a better picture of [the thing] and would value about 10 minutes of your time. It's a voice conversation you can do when it suits you, no meeting to book: {interview_link}
```

## Checklist

- [ ] **Test the link once yourself.** Open it and confirm you see the start screen. Leave without finishing, and do not count that session as one of your 5 interviews.
- [ ] **Choose people who recently did the thing you are studying.** Recent signups, active users, or people who cancelled will have a fresher story than a general list.
- [ ] **Send a personal note, not just the bare link.** Use one of the messages above, with your product and the right length filled in.
- [ ] **Invite 10 to 20 people if your goal is 5 interviews.** This is a rule of thumb, not measured Frank data. We do not have a reliable invite-to-interview rate to quote.
- [ ] **Follow up once, about 2 days later, with people who have not replied.** This is also a rule of thumb, not measured data. One reminder is enough.
- [ ] **Treat 5 completed interviews as a good first goal.** That is a practical starting bar: enough to see that the study works and to start hearing repeating themes. It is not a claim that 5 interviews are "enough research" for every decision. If you want a longer note on when to stop, see [How to run customer interviews](how-to-run-customer-interviews.md).

When answers start coming in, they show up in your Frank account as transcripts and themes. If the topic is off, you can edit the guide and send the same link again.
