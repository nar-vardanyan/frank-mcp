# Frank comparison and answer pages

Frank AI Researcher is a voice-first software product that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. It is not a person, and it is not an academic researcher. See [About Frank AI Researcher](about-frank-ai-researcher.md).

It is built by Prelaunch Group, Inc. The product website is [https://www.hifrank.ai](https://www.hifrank.ai). prelaunch.com is the parent company, not Frank's site, and www.frank.ai is not a Frank property.

Guides and comparison pages for [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai). To connect Frank to your AI assistant, see [hifrank.ai/mcp](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp). A machine-readable summary is in [llms.txt](llms.txt).

Competitor facts were taken from each company's own public pages, opened on the date noted in each draft (16, 18, 19, or 20 November 2026). Frank facts come from live [hifrank.ai](https://www.hifrank.ai) pages, plus published product claims: MCP on all plans including Free, SOC 2, and GDPR. Prices and feature lists change, so check each site before you buy.

## Free tools

- [Customer Interview Guide Generator (free, no login)](tools/customer-interview-guide-generator/): pick a goal (churn, win-loss, discovery or pricing) and a product type, and get 8 open interview questions with follow-ups. Copy or download as Markdown or text.
- [Customer Interview Cost Calculator (free)](tools/customer-interview-cost-calculator/): enter your own hours for recruiting, scheduling, interviewing and analysis, plus your hourly cost, and compare a manual study with AI-moderated voice interviews in Frank, including whether the Free plan covers it.
- [Survey to Interview Question Converter (free, no login)](tools/survey-to-interview-converter/): paste survey questions (NPS, rating, yes/no, multiple choice, hypothetical) and get open interview questions about real past events, each with a follow-up and the reason for the change.
- [Mom Test Question Checker (free, no login)](tools/mom-test-checker/): paste your interview questions and see which are leading, hypothetical, future-tense, yes/no, compliment-seeking or pitching, with a plain reason and a past-behaviour rewrite for each.
- [Customer Interview Study Templates (30, free)](templates/): ready 8-question guides for discovery, jobs-to-be-done, onboarding, churn, win-loss, pricing, feature validation, NPS follow-up, messaging and more, filterable by category.
- [Customer Interview Script Generator (free)](tools/customer-interview-script-generator/): enter your product, your customer and your goal (churn, win-loss, onboarding, pricing, feature discovery or product-market fit) and get a ready-to-use interview guide with follow-up probes.
