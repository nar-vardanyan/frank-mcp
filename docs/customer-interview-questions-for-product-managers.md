## When to use this guide

Use these question groups when you need to change a roadmap, a shipped feature, or onboarding, and you want evidence from a recent event, not opinions about a hypothetical product. Keep the groups that serve that decision, and cut the rest. Replace [product] and [feature] with the names users use. Invite users from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

## Questions: why a shipped feature is unused

Use this group when a feature is live and usage is lower than you expected, or when people tried it once and did not return.

1. Walk me through the last time you noticed [feature] in [product]. What were you trying to get done?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: What did you do instead, if you skipped it?
2. What almost made you try [feature], and what stopped you?
   Follow up: Was it timing, clarity, permission, or something else?
3. Tell me about a workaround you still use instead of [feature]. What does that workaround give you that [feature] does not?
   Follow up: What would have to be true for you to drop the workaround?

## Questions: what to build next

Use this group after a release cycle, when you are choosing between a few ideas and need to hear how people actually work today.

1. Walk me through the last time you tried to do [job] with [product]. Where did you get stuck?
   Follow up: What did you do next?
2. What have you added around [product], such as a spreadsheet, a script, or another tool, to finish the job?
   Follow up: Which of those add-ons would you most want [product] to replace?
3. If you could change one thing about [product] before next month, what would it be, and what happened recently that makes that change matter?
   Follow up: Who else on your team would feel that change?

## Questions: time to first value

Use this group when new accounts stall after signup, or when you want to tighten the first session.

1. Walk me through the first time you tried to get a real result from [product]. What were you hoping to finish that day?
   Follow up: How long did that take, and where did you hesitate?
2. What did you need to know, or have in place, before [product] became useful?
   Follow up: What was missing, and how did you work around it?
3. After you started, what took longer than you expected?
   Follow up: What would you tell a teammate to prepare before they start?

## FAQ

### What should a product manager ask in a customer interview?

Ask about a recent workflow, not a hypothetical feature. Start with why a shipped feature went unused, where people get stuck doing a real job, and what delayed first value. Use the question groups on this page, and cut any group that does not serve the decision you have to make.

### Does Frank recruit users for product interviews?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite users from your own product, CRM, or email list.

### When should I use the unused-feature group versus the roadmap group?

Use the unused-feature group when something is already live and people are skipping it. Use the roadmap group when you are choosing what to build next and need to hear current workarounds. You can keep both if one release created both questions.
