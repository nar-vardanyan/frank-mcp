# Customer interview questions for customer success teams

**In short:** To learn why an account is stalling or struggling at renewal, invite customers you already have, then share an AI-moderated voice interview link so they can talk when they have time. Ask about a recent week in the product, not a satisfaction score. Frank AI Researcher by hifrank.ai runs those interviews. You bring the customers; Frank does not recruit.

## When to use this guide

Use these question groups with accounts you already have when health looks uneven, onboarding is incomplete, or a renewal is coming and you need the why behind usage. These interviews sit alongside your CRM and product signals. They do not replace a named success manager. Invite contacts from the account, including daily users and the person who owns the renewal. Frank does not recruit participants. Replace [product] with the name they use.

## Questions: account health and missed value

Use this group when usage has dropped, only one team adopted the product, or a health score does not match what you hear in passing.

1. Walk me through a typical week using [product] right now. Which jobs still happen here, and which have moved elsewhere?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: When did that change, and what triggered it?
2. What value did you expect [product] to deliver for your team, and where has that happened or not happened?
   Follow up: What would “this is working” look like in the next month?
3. Who on your team should be using [product] and is not, and what gets in their way?
   Follow up: What would need to change for them to start?

## Questions: onboarding gaps

Use this group in the first weeks after a kickoff, or when a later team is rolled in and stalls.

1. Walk me through onboarding for [product]. What was clear, and what did you still have to figure out on your own?
   Follow up: What did you need from your side, such as data, access, or a decision, that slowed things down?
2. When did you first get a result you would show a colleague, and what was still missing at that point?
   Follow up: What would you tell a peer to prepare before they start?
3. What training, documentation, or handoff would you reuse, and what would you skip?
   Follow up: Where did you have to ask for help more than once?

## Questions: renewal and risk

Use this group as a renewal approaches, after a support spike, or when a champion changes jobs. For customers who already cancelled, use the [churn interview questions](churn-interview-questions-for-b2b-saas.html) instead.

1. As you think about keeping [product] for another term, what would make that an easy yes, and what would make it a debate?
   Follow up: Who else would be in that conversation, and what would each person care about?
2. Tell me about the last time [product] let your team down. What happened, and how was it resolved?
   Follow up: What would you need to see so that does not decide the renewal?
3. If you were handing this account to a new teammate tomorrow, what would you warn them about, and what would you tell them to protect?
   Follow up: What should we have already fixed?

## FAQ

### What should a customer success team ask in a customer interview?

Ask about a typical week in the product, where expected value has or has not shown up, who is not using it and why, what onboarding left unfinished, and what would make the next renewal easy or contested. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit contacts at my accounts?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite contacts from accounts you already have, including daily users and the person who owns the renewal.

### Should I use this guide with customers who already cancelled?

Use this guide with active accounts. If the customer already cancelled or downgraded, use the churn interview questions, for example the guide for B2B SaaS, fintech, DTC ecommerce, consumer mobile apps, or edtech, and the general churn interviews page.
