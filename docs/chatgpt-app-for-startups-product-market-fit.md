## Where this fits after discovery

Discovery interviews come before you build, and ask about a problem people already have. This page is for the next stage, once you have recent active users. Ask them the Sean Ellis question, "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?", with the answers very disappointed, somewhat disappointed, and not disappointed. The survey gives you groups. The interviews explain the main benefit, the alternative, and what is missing.

## Who to interview

- Recent active users, not people who only signed up or tried the product once.
- People who answered very disappointed, to learn who already needs the product and why.
- People who answered somewhat disappointed, to learn what holds them back.

Invite them by in-app message or email. Frank does not recruit participants.

## What to ask each group

**Very disappointed:** the last time they used the product, what they used before, the main benefit in their own words, who else it helps, and what they would use if it went away.

**Somewhat disappointed:** what they hoped for when they started, the last time it fell short, what they use alongside it, and what is missing.

```text
Use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it. I want to interview active users of [product] who said they would be very disappointed if they could no longer use it. Write 6 to 8 open questions about their last use: what they were doing, what they used before, the main benefit in their words, and what they would use if it went away. Wait for me to review the guide.
```

## Reading the result honestly

Interview counts describe the people you spoke with, not all users. Use the survey for the share and the interviews for the reasons. Treat compliments and predictions as weak evidence, and look for what people actually did.

```text
From Frank, compare the very disappointed and somewhat disappointed interviews in my study. Where do the benefits, use cases, and customer profiles differ? Only report differences backed by more than one interview, with quotes.
```

## What it does not do

- **No participant recruiting.** You invite your own active users.
- **Interviews, not the survey.** Ask the Sean Ellis question in your own survey or product, then invite people to the interview.
- **No prototype or image tests.** Frank does not show designs or mockups.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
