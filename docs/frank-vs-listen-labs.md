# Frank vs Listen Labs: AI-Moderated Interviews Compared

**In short:** Frank and Listen Labs both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts, themes, and quotes you can check against the original conversation. Choose Listen Labs if you need a recruited panel, video interviews with on-screen concept or prototype testing, 120+ interview languages, or the enterprise quality and security documentation Listen publishes; choose Frank if you want to interview customers you already have, starting from a free plan with published prices, with MCP included on every plan, including Free.

We make [Frank AI Researcher](https://www.hifrank.ai), a voice-first product from Prelaunch.com that runs AI-moderated voice interviews with your own customers. Listen Labs is a separate AI research platform. This page compares them from both companies' live public pages, opened on 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan). Prices and feature lists change, so check each site before you buy.

## When to choose which

**Choose Listen Labs** when you need people you do not already have. Listen says it can recruit from a global network of 50M+ participants, including hard-to-reach audiences, in 70+ countries, or you can bring your own contacts. It is also the better pick when the interview itself is a video session, when you need to show images, videos, URLs, or Figma or Lovable prototypes (with screensharing), when you want boardroom-ready slides and highlight reels, or when you need Listen's Quality Guard, which Listen says is on by default and will recruit a replacement at no extra cost if a response is flagged as low quality. Listen's own pages also document a broader language range than Frank's: a product announcement and the homepage say interviews and reporting run in 120+ languages. Listen's Trust Center and Quality Guard page state SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, ISO 42001, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701, plus optional EU data hosting.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you do not need a panel. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site), let voice conversations run (including overnight), and read the transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan with monthly voice minutes and no credit card, and MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Frank works today in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse, so you can set up a study and read transcripts from those assistants. Frank is SOC 2 Type II and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security). If you want many consistent voice conversations with your own customers, and you want to start from a published price list rather than a platform-fee quote, that is the fit.

**Either tool can work** if you only need to invite people you already know, run interviews without a calendar, and read themes linked back to quotes. Both document an MCP connector, and both state SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance. Those overlaps are why the choice usually comes down to recruiting, interview format (Frank is voice-first; Listen's user guide describes video interviews), language coverage, deliverables, and how pricing is published, not to whether both can run an AI-moderated interview.

## How Frank works

You set a research goal and an interview guide. Frank suggests topics from that goal, then runs the voice interviews for you. You invite your own customers with a shareable interviewer link, or you embed a website widget so someone can start from your site. People join when they have time. There is no calendar to manage, and interviews can run in parallel, including overnight.

After each conversation you get a transcript. Answers are grouped into themes you can trace back to the original quotes. If you already work in an AI assistant, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) and ask it to help draft a study or read transcripts without leaving that chat. New research is created as a draft, so it is not published to participants until you approve it.

Frank can run hundreds of interviews overnight with customers you can already reach. It does not find those people for you. If you have no customers yet, you will need another way to recruit. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews) in a cancellation flow, [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery).

## How Listen Labs works

On Listen's site, you describe a research goal and Listen drafts objectives, questions, and probing context, or you upload your own guide. You then either ask Listen to recruit or share a link with people you already have. The user guide says first completes typically arrive within minutes for general-population audiences and within hours for targeted audiences, with longer fielding times for niche professional groups.

The AI moderator holds the conversation and asks follow-ups inside the objectives and topic boundaries you approve. Workspace rules can block sensitive topics and brand-risk language. Quality Guard runs on every study. After the interviews, Listen builds reports, decks, and highlight reels, and a Research Agent can answer questions with cited quotes. You can also import interviews or files you collected elsewhere into the workspace library.

Listen MCP is documented so tools such as Claude, Cursor, or Notion can query that library. The announcement says existing customers should contact their account team for access ([Listen MCP](https://listenlabs.com/blog/listen-mcp), as of Nov 18, 2026).

## FAQ

### What is the difference between Frank and Listen Labs?
Both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts and themes. The main difference is the job each one is set up to do. Listen Labs can recruit participants, runs video interviews with concept and prototype testing, publishes a wider language range, and sells through a platform fee plus credits. Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you already have, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Frank does not recruit.

### When should I choose Listen Labs?
Choose Listen Labs when you need a recruited panel, when the interview itself is a video session or has to show a prototype or other stimulus, when you want 120+ interview and reporting languages, when you need Quality Guard's replacement guarantee, or when you need the ISO certifications and optional EU hosting Listen documents. Those capabilities are documented on Listen's own site and are the cases where Listen is the more specific fit.

### When should I choose Frank?
Choose Frank when you can already reach the customers you want to hear from and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a link or website widget, including overnight. Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, and states SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance. It is a weaker fit if you need recruiting, video or on-screen prototype testing, Listen's language coverage, or the additional ISO and EU-hosting documentation Listen lists.

### Does Frank recruit interview participants?
No. Frank does not recruit participants. Invite your own customers: people who recently signed up, cancelled, bought, or used the feature. If you have no customers yet, a recruiting service or your network is a better first step. Listen Labs can recruit through its participant network or work with people you bring.

### Do both tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Yes. Frank is available as an [MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp) on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct and Muse (tested), plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth. New Frank research is created as a draft and is not published to participants without your approval. Listen documents an MCP connector for Claude, Notion, Figma, ChatGPT, Cursor, Microsoft Copilot, and other MCP-compatible tools, but the announcement says existing customers should contact their account team for access. Listen does not state on the pages reviewed whether MCP is included on every plan.

### How does Frank pricing compare with Listen Labs?
As of 18 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan), Frank's Free plan includes 60 voice minutes per month with no credit card, and paid plans start at $59 per month, or $49 per month billed yearly. Listen Labs does not publish a public dollar price list. Its user guide describes a platform fee plus interview credits that vary by audience. Prices change; check [Frank's pricing page](https://www.hifrank.ai/pricing) and ask Listen Labs for a current quote before you buy.

### How do Frank and Listen Labs handle security?
Frank is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant. The live security page also states AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, with access controls you manage. Listen Labs states SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA compliance, plus ISO 42001, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701, 256-bit encryption, and optional EU data hosting. Listen says it never uses customer data to train AI models. Both companies document these claims on their own live pages as of 18 November 2026.
