## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand a completed order, a left cart, a return, or why someone did or did not buy again. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [brand] with the name customers use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite customers from your own order, cart, or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you bought from [brand], or almost bought and left the cart. What were you trying to get?
2. What almost stopped you from placing the order?
3. Walk me through what happened after you paid: delivery, a question, or a problem.
4. Tell me about a return or an exchange. What triggered it, and how did it go?
5. What made you come back for another order, or kept you from coming back?
6. How do promotions, shipping, or what arrived affect whether you order again?
7. What would make you choose this brand over another one next time, or the other way around?
8. If you could change one thing about buying from this brand, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a DTC ecommerce customer interview?

Ask about a recent order, a left cart, a return, or a repeat purchase: what they were trying to get, what almost stopped the order, what happened after they paid, and what would make them buy again. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit ecommerce customers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite customers from your own order, cart, or email list.

### When should I use this purchase and return guide?

Use it when you want to change checkout, post-purchase support, returns, or the reason people buy again. Cut or rewrite questions that do not serve that decision, and keep a consistent core so you can compare answers.
