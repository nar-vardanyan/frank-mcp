How to write a useful interview summary

  • Write it the same day. Details and exact wording fade fast, so a short write-up right after the call beats a long one later.
  • Keep what they said apart from what you think. Quotes go in Quotes, word for word. Your reading of them goes in Key insights.
  • Tie every insight to evidence. If you can't point to a quote or a story, it is a hunch: put it in Open questions instead.
  • Use the same sections every time. Comparing many summaries is easy when they share a shape, and themes across interviews stand out.
  • End with actions. A summary nobody acts on is a diary. Each next step needs an owner and a date.
  • Keep it anonymous when you share it. Use a participant code instead of a name, and leave out details that identify the person.
