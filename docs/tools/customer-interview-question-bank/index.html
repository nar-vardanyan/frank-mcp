How to use the question bank

  • Start with the goal. Pick discovery, churn, onboarding, retention, feature feedback, pricing or win-loss to see only the questions that fit.
  • Pick a few per stage. A good guide moves from warm-up, to how they work today, to a real example of the problem, to what they tried, and ends with wrap-up questions.
  • Keep it short. Fewer questions leave room for follow-ups, and follow-ups are where the useful answers come from.
  • Ask about the past, not the future. "Tell me about the last time" gets real behavior; "would you use" gets polite guesses.
  • Check your own questions. Paste any question you add yourself into the Mom Test question checker.

FAQ

What is a customer interview question bank?

A list of tried interview questions you can choose from instead of writing a guide from scratch. This one lets you filter by goal and interview stage, pick the questions you want, and copy them as your own guide with a follow-up for each.

What makes a good customer interview question?

It asks about something that really happened, in the person's own words: "Tell me about the last time" rather than "Would you use". Good questions are open, don't hint at the answer you want, and leave room for a follow-up. Every question here passes the Mom Test question checker.

How is this different from the interview guide generator?

The guide generator gives you a ready-made guide for one goal. The question bank shows every question across goals and stages so you can mix and pick your own, for example onboarding questions with a few pricing ones.

Can I share my picks with my team?

Yes. Copy link to these picks makes a link with the goal, stage and question numbers. Your product name and topic are not put in the link.

Does this use AI? Is anything sent to a server?

No. The questions are a fixed list and the filters run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
