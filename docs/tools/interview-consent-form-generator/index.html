Interview consent form: Acme Invoicing customer research

What this is

We, Acme, are talking to customers to learn how you use Acme Invoicing and what we could do better. This is research, not a sales call.

What you'll do

You'll have a conversation of about 20 minutes. [AI interviewer name], an AI interviewer, will ask the questions out loud and follow up on your answers. It is not a person. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question.

What we keep

We keep an audio recording and a written transcript of the conversation. Recording starts only after you agree, and we tell you when it starts.

Who sees it and for how long

Only the people on our team who work on Acme Invoicing can see it. We keep it for [how long you keep it], then delete it. We don't sell it, and we use it only for the purpose above.

How we use what you say

We may quote your words in internal reports without your name. We'll ask you separately before using anything that could identify you outside our team.

Taking part is up to you. You can stop at any time, during or after the conversation, and ask us to delete what we kept, without giving a reason. Saying no won't change how we treat you as a customer.

Contact [contact email] with any question or to ask us to delete your data.

What an interview consent form should cover

  • Who you are and why you're asking. One or two plain sentences, and a clear line that this is research, not a sales call.
  • What taking part involves. How long it takes, who asks the questions, and that people can skip any question.
  • What you keep. Audio, a transcript or notes, and when recording starts.
  • Who sees it and for how long. A named group, a real retention period, and what happens after it.
  • How you use what people say. Internal quotes without names, and a separate ask before anything public.
  • Their choices. Taking part is voluntary, they can stop at any time, and they can ask you to delete their data.
  • A contact. A real person or inbox that answers.
  • Separate tick boxes. One for taking part, one for recording and one for quotes, so people can agree to one without the others.

Recording disclosure: what to say at the start of the call

Even with a signed form, say it out loud before you record: who you are, what the call is for, that it is recorded, who sees it and for how long, and that they can stop. Then ask for a clear yes, and start recording only after it. The generator writes this script for you. Some teams also start the recording right after the yes and ask the question again, so the agreement is on the recording.

Telling people an AI is asking the questions

If an AI interviewer runs the conversation, say so in the invite, in the form and at the start of the call. People answer more openly when nothing about the setup surprises them. Tips

  • Only promise what you do. If you keep recordings for 12 months, don't write 30 days. Edit any line that isn't true for you.
  • Keep the yes. Store the signed form or the recorded agreement with the interview, and delete both when the retention period ends.
  • Get advice for special cases. People under 18, health or financial details, employees of a customer and interviews across borders can all need extra steps. Ask your counsel first.
  • Make thank-yous unconditional. If you offer one, people get it whether or not they answer every question.

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. It writes template text that you edit. Consent and recording rules differ by place and situation, so check the final text with your legal counsel and your local rules before you use it.

Does using this form make my research compliant?

No. No template can do that on its own. What matters is that the text is true for your study and that you do what it says: ask before recording, keep data only as long as you said, and delete it when asked.

Do I need consent to record an interview?

Rules differ by place, and some require every person on the call to agree before recording. The safest habit is to tell people before you start, ask for a clear yes, and record only after it. Ask your counsel what applies to you.

Should I tell people an AI is asking the questions?

We think so. Say it in the invite, in the form and at the start of the call. Choose "An AI interviewer" and the generator adds a plain line to every part of the output.
