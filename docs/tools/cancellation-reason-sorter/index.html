Cancellation Reason Sorter

Paste the answers from your cancellation or churn survey and see them grouped into clear themes, with counts, percentages and real examples for each. Then get interview follow-up questions that find out what actually happened behind each reason. Free, no login: it runs in your browser and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

How it sorts: by keyword and phrase rules, not AI. It can miss nuance, sarcasm and unusual wording, so read the examples. Answers that match no rule go to Unclear instead of being forced into a theme, and an answer that names two reasons counts in both.

How the sorting works

  • Phrase rules, per clause. Each answer is split at commas, full stops and words like "but" and "just", and each part is checked against the phrase lists above.
  • Negation. A phrase after "not", "never", "don't" and similar words is ignored, so "not too expensive, just unused" counts as Not using it enough only. A phrase followed by "was fine" or "wasn't the issue" is ignored too.
  • No force-fitting. Vague answers such as "n/a" or "not worth it" go to Unclear. Those are often the best people to interview.
  • Counts are of your answers only. Percentages are shares of the answers you pasted, not benchmarks. An answer with two reasons counts in both themes.

Why follow up with an interview

"Too expensive" can mean the price went up, the budget was cut, a cheaper tool did enough, or the product never delivered enough value to justify any price. A survey answer can't tell those apart. A short interview about what happened, in order, can. The follow-up questions ask about real past events rather than opinions or hypotheticals, and each one passes the rules of our Mom Test question checker. FAQ

Does this use AI?

No. It sorts answers with fixed keyword and phrase rules that run in your browser. The same answers always give the same result. That also means it can miss nuance, so check the example answers in each theme.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored. The page makes no network requests when you sort, copy or download.

Why are the themes the same as Stripe's?

Stripe records a cancellation reason in cancellation_details.feedback with the values too_expensive, missing_features, switched_service, unused, customer_service, too_complex, low_quality and other. Using the same themes lets you compare free-text answers with what your billing data already shows.

Why is an answer in Unclear?

No rule matched it, or it was too vague, such as "n/a" or "not worth it". The sorter never guesses. Unclear answers are often the best ones to follow up with an interview.

Can one answer be in two themes?

Yes. "Switched to HubSpot, it was cheaper" counts as both Switched to another product and Too expensive, so the percentages can add up to more than 100%.

What can I export?

A CSV with every answer, its themes, the Stripe value and the phrase that matched, and a Markdown report with the counts, examples and follow-up questions.

Does Frank find people to interview?

No. Frank does not recruit participants. You invite customers you already have, such as the ones who just cancelled.

Can I set up the interview from an AI assistant?

Yes. Frank has a remote MCP server at https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp, included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.
