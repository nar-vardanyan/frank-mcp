How the calculator works

Every hour and dollar figure comes from the fields above. The page does not add industry averages, and the values it opens with are only examples.

  • Manual hours = setup + interviews × (recruiting + scheduling + interviewing + analysis).
  • Hours with Frank = setup + interviews × (recruiting + your review hours). Scheduling and live interviewing are zero, because participants open the interview link when it suits them and Frank moderates.
  • Cost of time = hours × your hourly cost.
  • Incentives = interviews × incentive per participant, in both columns.
  • Frank plan cost = one month of the plan you pick. Voice minutes needed = interviews × minutes per interview.

Method notes

  • Recruiting stays the same. Frank does not recruit participants. You still find and invite the people, so those hours and any incentive appear in both columns.
  • Review is not zero. Frank returns transcripts, summaries and themes, but you should still read them and check each theme against the quotes. Enter the time that takes you.
  • Voice minutes are an estimate. Frank counts the minutes interviews actually use, and real interviews run shorter or longer than planned. If you go over a plan's minutes, hifrank.ai/pricing says you can keep interviewing at that plan's overage rate; this calculator does not price overage.
  • One month. The plan check assumes the whole study runs in one month. If you spread it over several months, each month's minutes count against that month's allowance.
  • Not counted: tools you already pay for, calendar time until results arrive, and the value of what you learn.

FAQ

Where do the numbers in this calculator come from?

From you. The calculator has no built-in benchmarks or industry averages. The values shown when the page opens are example values only, so replace them with your own. The only fixed numbers are Frank's published plan prices and voice minutes, taken from hifrank.ai/pricing on 26 November 2026.

Does Frank recruit participants?

No. Frank does not recruit participants. You invite people you already have, such as customers, users or your email list. That is why recruiting time and incentives are the same in both columns.

What does Frank remove from the manual approach?

Scheduling and live moderating. Participants open one interview link when it suits them, and Frank asks the questions out loud, follows up on the answers, and runs many interviews in parallel. Frank also returns transcripts, summaries and themes, but you still read and check them, so the calculator asks for your own review hours instead of assuming zero.

When does the Frank Free plan cover my study?

The Free plan includes 60 voice minutes a month with no credit card, and interviews up to 10 minutes long. If your number of interviews times minutes per interview is 60 or less, and each interview is 10 minutes or shorter, the study fits in one month of the Free plan. Otherwise the calculator shows which paid plan's monthly minutes and interview length cover it.

Is anything I type stored or sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser. If you copy the share link, your inputs are in that link's address, and only the people you send it to will see them.

Can I set up the study from an AI assistant?

Yes. Frank has a remote MCP server at https://api.hifrank.ai/mcp, included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude and Muse, plus other MCP clients that support remote servers with OAuth.
