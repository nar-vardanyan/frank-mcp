## How to interview churned customers

1. Decide what you will change, for example an onboarding step or a cancel-flow message.
2. Write a short guide about what happened: when they first signed up, when they started to doubt the product, what they tried, and what they use now.
3. Invite people who recently cancelled, from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.
4. Share an interviewer link, or embed a website widget, so they can talk when they have time, without booking a call.
5. Read the transcripts. Group stories by trigger, and check any theme you will act on against exact quotes.

A cancellation survey is still useful for counting reasons. Interviews are for the story behind a short reason code. For a longer method walkthrough, see [How to find out why customers churn](how-to-find-out-why-customers-churn.html) and [How to run churn interviews with ChatGPT and Frank MCP](how-to-run-churn-interviews-with-chatgpt-and-frank-mcp.html).
