## Discovery before you build

Good discovery interviews ask about the last time someone dealt with a problem: what happened, what they did, what they tried, and what it cost them. They do not ask whether people like your idea, because opinions and predictions are weak evidence. This is the approach behind [The Mom Test](glossary/the-mom-test.html). Frank's AI interviewer follows the guide you approve, so the guide is where that discipline lives.

## If you do not have customers yet

Frank does not recruit participants, so you need people you can reach. Founders usually start with:

- People in their own network who have the problem.
- A waitlist or early sign-up list.
- Communities they already belong to, following each group's rules.

Describe who you want by behavior, for example "handled invoicing for a small agency in the last month", rather than by job title alone.

## Turn assumptions into a study

**Draft a study for the riskiest assumption**

```text
My idea depends on these assumptions: [list]. Pick the riskiest one. Then use Frank to create a draft study, and do not publish it, about the last time [customer type] dealt with [problem]: what triggered it, what they did, what they tried, and what it cost them. Do not mention my idea or any solution. Wait for me to review the guide.
```

**Score assumptions after the interviews**

```text
From Frank, read the transcripts in my discovery study. For each of my assumptions, show quotes that support it and quotes that contradict it, and say whether the evidence is strong (past behavior, money or time spent) or weak (opinions, predictions).
```

## When to talk to people yourself

For deep discovery on a messy problem, run some interviews yourself as well. AI interviewers follow the script and probe when answers are short, and they work well as a complement to human moderation. Use Frank to hear from more people than your calendar allows, and your own calls to chase what surprises you.

## What it does not do

- **No participant recruiting.** Frank does not find interviewees. You invite people you can reach.
- **No idea validation by itself.** It collects interviews; deciding what the evidence means is still your step.
- **No prototype or image tests.** Frank does not show mockups or designs.
- **Voice interviews only.** No video, WhatsApp, or text chat interviews.
