## What "cheap" should include

Price per interview is only part of the cost. A low-cost week also needs:

1. **People you can already reach.** Email, in-app messages, or a link on your site. If you have to buy a panel, the week is no longer cheap, and Frank is the wrong product for that job.
2. **Time to invite, not time to moderate.** If you have to sit on 10 live calls, the hidden cost is your calendar, not the software.
3. **A usable transcript.** Notes you typed during a call are cheaper up front and more expensive later, when you cannot search or share them.
4. **No contract and no card**, so you can run the week without a procurement delay.

## A 5-day plan for 10 interviews

### Day 1: Pick one question and a list of 20 people

Pick one decision you need to make this week, such as why a trial user did not finish setup, or why a cancelled customer left. Invite about 20 people from your own list so that 10 complete interviews is realistic. If you stay on the Free plan, write a short guide so each interview can stay at about 5 minutes. A short [customer interview questions template](customer-interview-questions-template.md) is a starting point you can cut down.

### Day 2: Publish a short voice interview

Create a study, attach your questions, and share a single interviewer link or website widget. Set the interview length to about 5 minutes if you want 10 interviews on the Free plan this week, or move to a paid plan for longer ones. Frank runs the interview, including follow-up questions, and returns a transcript and themes. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse. You can draft the study from a chat if you already use one of those assistants. Setup is on the [MCP connector page](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/frank-mcp-connector?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=kpi4b&utm_content=cheapest-way-to-run-10-customer-interviews-this-week).

### Days 3 and 4: Send reminders, do not add new questions

One reminder is usually enough. Do not rewrite the guide mid-week, or you will not be able to compare the 10 transcripts.

### Day 5: Read all 10 transcripts and write three themes

Read the transcripts, not only the summary. Write three themes you can act on, each tied to a quote. If a theme appears once, keep it as an anecdote, not a finding.

## When a different approach is cheaper

If you only need one number, a one-question survey can be cheaper than 10 interviews. If you need to watch someone use a screen, you need a usability tool, not a voice interviewer. If you have no one to invite, you need recruiting or a panel, which Frank does not provide.
