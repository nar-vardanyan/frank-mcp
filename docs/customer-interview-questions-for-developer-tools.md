## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when you need to understand install, the first successful use, team buy-in, or a switch. Keep all eight questions if they serve that decision, or cut the ones that do not. Replace [product] with the name developers use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite users from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you installed or tried [product]. What were you trying to get done?
2. Walk me through the first time you got it working. Where did you get stuck?
3. What docs, examples, or error messages did you use, and what was missing?
4. How does it fit into your existing stack, and what do you still do outside it?
5. Who else on the team had to agree, and what did they care about?
6. Tell me about a time you almost switched away, or did switch. What triggered that?
7. What would make you recommend this tool to another team, or warn them off it?
8. If you could change one thing about getting from install to daily use, what would it be?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a developer-tools customer interview?

Ask about a recent install or first successful use: what they were trying to get done, where they got stuck, how it fits the stack, who else had to agree, and what would make them switch. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit developers?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite users from your own product or email list.

### When should I use this install and switching guide?

Use it when you want to change onboarding after install, docs and errors, team buy-in, or the moment someone considers switching. Cut or rewrite questions that do not serve that decision, and keep a consistent core so you can compare answers.
