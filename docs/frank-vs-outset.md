## When to choose which

**Choose Outset** when you need people you do not already have. Outset recruits through 25+ native panel integrations, and its recruit page says you can reach over 1.1 billion global participants in over 85 countries, or you can bring your own list. It is also the better pick when the interview itself has to be video, text, or voice-to-voice, when a researcher needs to run on-screen usability or prototype tests (including Figma files, live websites, or mobile apps, with picture-in-picture screen sharing), when you want to interview digital twins grounded in prior human interviews, or when you want to lead a human interview yourself in the same platform, with a backroom for teammates. Outset also states HIPAA compliance and ISO 42001 certification alongside SOC 2 Type II and GDPR, and it offers custom pricing plus optional managed support for study setup and recruitment.

**Choose Frank** when you already have customers to invite and you do not need a panel. Frank does not recruit participants. You share an [interviewer link](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/navigating-your-ai-interviewer-dashboard?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset) or a [website widget](https://www.hifrank.ai/docs/website-widget-embedding-frank-on-your-site?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset), let voice conversations run, and read the transcripts and themes. Frank publishes a Free plan with monthly voice minutes and no credit card, and MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse. Frank is SOC 2 and [GDPR compliant](https://www.hifrank.ai/features/transparency-security?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset). Frank also supports multilingual interviews; Outset lists 40+ languages. If you want many consistent voice conversations with your own customers, rather than a recruited or multimodal study, that is the fit.

**Either tool can work** if you need to share a link with people you already know, run interviews without a calendar, and read themes linked back to quotes. Both document an MCP connector, and both state SOC 2 and GDPR compliance. Those overlaps are why the choice usually comes down to recruiting, interview format (Frank is voice-first; Outset also runs video, text, and on-screen testing), plan structure, and how much security documentation you need, not to whether both can run an AI-moderated interview.

## How Frank works

You set a research goal and an interview guide. Frank suggests topics from that goal, then runs the voice interviews for you. You invite your own customers with a shareable interviewer link, or you embed a website widget so someone can start from your site. People join when they have time. There is no calendar to manage, and interviews can run in parallel.

After each conversation you get a transcript. Answers are grouped into themes you can trace back to the original quotes. If you already work in an AI assistant, you can [connect Frank as an MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset) and ask it to help draft a study or read transcripts without leaving that chat. New research is created as a draft, so it is not published to participants until you approve it.

Frank does not find participants for you. If you have no customers yet, you will need another way to recruit. Typical uses include [churn interviews](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-churn-interviews?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset) in a cancellation flow, [win-loss](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/win-loss-analysis?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset), and [product discovery](https://www.hifrank.ai/use-case/ai-product-discovery?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset).

## How Outset works

On Outset's site, you set up an interview guide from your research goals (Outset can generate the guide, or you can bring your own), then either recruit through Outset's panel integrations or share a link with people you already have. Outset's AI interviewer then runs the conversations (in text, voice, video, or voice-to-voice) and can probe in real time based on what it hears and sees.

After the sessions, Outset synthesizes themes, quotes, and highlight reels, and you can chat with the data. The interviews page also describes visual and on-screen studies: the moderator can see click paths and facial reactions, and can run usability tests on Figma files, prototypes, or live apps and sites. Outset also documents Digital Twins, built from AI-moderated human interviews, and Live Interviews, where a researcher leads the conversation in the same platform and teammates can listen from a backroom.

A hosted MCP connector is documented so a team can launch studies, analyze results, or chat with digital twins from tools such as Claude or Cursor. Plan-by-plan MCP inclusion is not documented on the Outset pages reviewed ([Platform](https://outset.ai/platform), [Outset MCP](https://outset.ai/resources/changeblog/outset-mcp), 20 Nov 2026).

## FAQ

### Is Frank an Outset alternative for interviewing my own customers?

Yes, if you already have customers to invite and you want voice interviews. Frank AI Researcher runs AI-moderated voice interviews from a shareable link or website widget and returns transcripts and themes. It has a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month), published paid prices, and MCP on every plan, including Free.

### Does Outset recruit participants?

Yes. Outset's recruit page describes 25+ native panel integrations and over 1.1 billion global participants in over 85 countries, or you can bring your own list. Frank does not recruit participants.

### Does Outset publish a starting price?

No public starting dollar price. Outset's pricing page offers custom pricing. Frank publishes a Free plan (225 signup credits plus 60 voice minutes a month) and paid plans starting at $49/month billed yearly.

### Is MCP included on Frank's Free plan?

Yes. MCP is included on every plan, including Free. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse.

### What is the difference between Frank and Outset?
Both run AI-moderated interviews and return transcripts and themes. The main difference is the job each one is set up to do. Outset can recruit participants, run video and on-screen usability or prototype tests, offer digital twins and researcher-led live interviews, and sells through custom pricing. Frank is built for voice interviews with customers you already have, with a free plan and MCP on every plan, including Free. Frank does not recruit.

### When should I choose Outset?
Choose Outset when you need a recruited panel, when interviews must be video, text, or voice-to-voice, when people must react to a prototype, Figma file, or live site during the session, when you want digital twins or researcher-led live interviews in the same platform, or when you need the HIPAA and ISO 42001 documentation Outset publishes. Those capabilities are documented on Outset's own site and are the cases where Outset is the more specific fit.

### When should I choose Frank?
Choose Frank when you can already reach the customers you want to hear from and you want AI-moderated voice interviews from a link or website widget. Frank publishes a Free plan, includes MCP on every plan, including Free, supports multilingual interviews, and states SOC 2 and GDPR compliance. It is a weaker fit if you need recruiting, video or on-screen prototype testing, digital twins, researcher-led live interviews, or the additional HIPAA and ISO 42001 claims Outset lists.

### Do both tools work with AI assistants like Claude?
Yes. Frank is available as an [MCP server](https://www.hifrank.ai/mcp?utm_source=frank-mcp-docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=frank-vs-outset) on all plans, including Free, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Muse. New Frank research is created as a draft and is not published to participants without your approval. Outset documents native MCP support for launching studies, analyzing results, and chatting with digital twins, and names Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible tool (the MCP changelog also names ChatGPT, Figma, Notion, and Glean). Plan-by-plan inclusion for Outset MCP is not documented. Help docs opened 20 November 2026 say official connectors currently work with the US deployment only.

### How do Frank and Outset handle security?
Frank is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant. The live security page also states AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, with access controls you manage. Outset is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, and also states ISO 42001 and HIPAA compliance, encryption at rest, TLS 1.3+ in transit, and SSO. Outset says it does not train on customer data. Both companies document these claims on their own live pages as of 20 November 2026.
