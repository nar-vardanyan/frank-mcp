## When to use this guide

Use these question groups when you need to understand a recent attempt to complete a task, a first session, or the context around a workflow. Frank runs voice interviews. It does not collect images, and it does not run prototype or video sessions. Invite people from your own product, research panel, or email list. Frank does not recruit participants. Replace [product] and [task] with the words participants use.

## Questions: a recent task and where it broke

Use this group after a change to a core flow, or when support tickets and session recordings point to a drop-off you cannot explain from the screen alone.

1. Walk me through the last time you tried to [task] in [product]. What were you trying to finish?
   Follow up if the first answer is thin: Where did you start, and what did you click or look for first?
2. At what point did you hesitate, back up, or leave the flow?
   Follow up: What did you think had gone wrong at that moment?
3. What did you do after you got stuck? Did you finish the task another way?
   Follow up: What would have helped you continue in [product]?

## Questions: first use and mental model

Use this group with people who signed up or were invited recently, when you want to hear how they made sense of [product] before they learned your labels.

1. Walk me through the first time you opened [product]. What did you think it was for?
   Follow up: What did you expect to be able to do in the first few minutes?
2. What felt familiar, and what felt unclear, in that first session?
   Follow up: Which words or labels did not match how you describe the job?
3. When did you first feel sure you had (or had not) succeeded?
   Follow up: What signal told you that?

## Questions: context of use

Use this group when the product is used at work or in a busy moment, and you need the constraints around the task, not only the screens.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [product] as part of a larger job. What else was going on?
   Follow up: Who else was involved, and what were you waiting on?
2. Where were you, and what device or setup were you using, when you last needed [product] to work quickly?
   Follow up: What would have made that moment easier?
3. What do you still keep outside [product] to finish the job, such as notes, a spreadsheet, or a message to a teammate?
   Follow up: Why does that part still live outside?

## FAQ

### What should a UX researcher ask in a customer interview?

Ask the person to walk through a recent attempt to complete a task, including where they hesitated, how they knew they had succeeded, and what they still do outside the product. Use the question groups on this page as a starting guide, and cut groups that do not serve the study goal.

### Does Frank recruit participants for UX research?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite people from your own product, research panel, or email list.

### Can I use Frank for prototype or image tests?

No. Frank is voice-first. It runs AI-moderated voice interviews with people you invite. It does not collect images, and it does not run prototype or video sessions.
