## Sample 8-question guide

Use this guide when a workflow spans clinicians, staff, and patients, and you need to see where the handoff breaks. Interview one group at a time with the same core questions, and note which role each person has. Replace [product] with the name they use, and add a follow-up under any question where the first answer is thin. Invite people from your own product or email list. Frank does not recruit participants.

1. Tell me about the last time you used [product] during a real patient encounter or workday. What were you trying to get done?
2. Walk me through who does which steps: clinician, staff, and patient.
3. Where does the workflow break, or get handed off badly, between those groups?
4. What did you do before this product, and what do you still do outside it?
5. Tell me about a time the product got in the way of care, or added work you did not expect.
6. What almost made you stop using it, or use it only for some patients or tasks?
7. What would need to be true for the other group (clinicians, staff, or patients) to use it the way you hoped?
8. If you could change one step in the workflow, which step and why?

## FAQ

### What should I ask in a healthtech customer interview?

Ask about a recent encounter or workday: who does each step, where the handoff between clinicians, staff, and patients breaks, when the product got in the way, and which step they would change. Use the eight questions on this page as a starting guide.

### Does Frank recruit clinicians or patients?

No. Frank AI Researcher does not recruit participants. You invite clinicians, staff, or patients from your own product or email list.

### Should I interview clinicians and patients with the same guide?

Use the same core questions so you can compare answers, and interview one group at a time. Note which role each person has, and follow up on the steps that group actually performs. Adapt wording so it matches their work, not your internal names.
